    Sewer Line Backups in Lawton, OK

    Sewage coming up a floor drain or the lowest tub in the house almost always means the main line is blocked. Stop running water. We open the outside cleanout, clear the line with a cable machine, then run a camera to see whether roots, grease or a broken joint caused it. We show you the video.

    Frozen Pipe Thawing and Repair in Lawton, OK

    Many homes were not built with deep cold in mind, so pipes in attics, exterior walls and garages are particularly vulnerable. Open the faucet before thawing anything. We warm frozen pipe slowly with heat guns and pipe heaters, never a torch, working from the faucet end back toward the ice so pressure has somewhere to go. If the line already split, we cut out the damaged length and replace it, then suggest insulation or heat cable for that run.

    Emergency Sump Pump Service in Lawton

    A basement pump that quits halfway through a heavy rain can let several inches of water in within an hour. Common causes are a stuck float switch, a burned-out motor or a check valve that let go. We check the float, motor and check valve and swaps the pump if needed. Then we trace the discharge line outside. A frozen or crushed discharge pipe sends water right back in.

    Old Pipe Failures in Lawton

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, which means the outside looks fine while the wall is nearly gone. When one joint lets go, the next one is often close behind. We cut back to sound pipe, transition to copper or PEX with a dielectric fitting, and give you a straight read on how much galvanized you still have. Pinhole leaks in old copper get the same treatment. We mark weak spots on the invoice.

    Pinhole and Fitting Leaks in Lawton

    Pressure above 80 PSI wears on every fitting in the house. Aggressive water can also eat pinholes in copper, which start as a mist and open into a spray. We shut the water, cut out the bad section and replace it with copper or PEX, and check static pressure at a hose bib with a gauge. Bad pressure regulators turn up often.

    Main Line Emergencies in Lawton

    Gurgling toilets and a slow tub together usually mean the main is closing up. Roots, grease and broken clay or cast iron are the usual suspects.
