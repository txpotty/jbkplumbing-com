    Backflow Preventer and Irrigation Freeze Repair in Lawton

    Backflow preventers and irrigation valves sit above ground and are exposed to the coldest air. Frozen backflow assemblies can crack their bodies or bonnets, causing leaks and failed tests. We repair or replace the assembly, insulate it with a fitted cover and recommend draining when deep cold is forecast. Many water providers require a passing backflow test after repairs, and we can arrange that.

    Residential Frozen Pipe Thawing in Lawton

    For homeowners in Lawton, a frozen pipe can be a major headache, often leading to a complete loss of water supply or, worse, a burst pipe. Our team uses non-invasive methods, such as controlled heat application or warm water jetting, to safely thaw your residential plumbing.     Hose Bib and Outdoor Faucet Repair in Lawton, OK

    Outdoor faucets freeze quickly, especially with a hose left connected. A split often hides inside the wall and only shows when the faucet is turned on in spring. We replace damaged hose bibs with frost-free models from brands like Woodford and Prier that shut off water inside the warm part of the wall. Insulated covers add more protection on cold nights.

    Preventative Pipe Winterization in Lawton

    Prevention is key to avoiding frozen pipes in Lawton. We offer professional winterization services, especially for homes that might be vacant or have vulnerable plumbing. This includes draining irrigation systems, insulating exposed pipes in attics or crawl spaces, and advising on proper faucet dripping during cold snaps. We can also install heat trace cables on critical lines to provide active protection against freezing, giving you peace of mind when temperatures drop.

    Attic and Exterior Wall Pipe Repair in Lawton

    Many homes run supply lines through attics or crawl spaces to reach bathrooms and kitchens, and those spaces get as cold as the outside air at night. Pipes in exterior walls with thin insulation are also at risk. We repair freeze-damaged sections and add proper insulation or reroute lines into conditioned space. These changes sharply reduce the odds of a repeat freeze.

    Frozen Pipe Thawing in Lawton

    When a faucet only trickles or nothing comes out after a cold night, ice is blocking the line somewhere. We open a faucet downstream to relieve pressure, then locate the frozen section with thermal imaging. Copper and galvanized pipes can be thawed with an electric pipe thawing machine that warms the metal from the inside. PEX and CPVC are warmed slowly with heat guns or infrared heaters to avoid softening the plastic.
