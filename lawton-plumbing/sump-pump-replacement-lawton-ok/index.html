    Which Sump Pump Suits Your Lawton, OK Home

    Pedestal Pump Service in Lawton, OK

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals. We replace worn units and set the float rod stops for a proper cycle. If your pit is wide enough, a submersible may serve you better.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Lawton, OK

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. They are louder, no question. The new column gets set plumb and braced, and we set the float rod stops so the pump turns on and off at the right water levels. They fit pits as narrow as 10 inches.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Lawton

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. First we time the pump cycles and measure the pit refill rate. From there it is math. Often a 1/2 or 3/4 HP pump with more GPM at your lift, plus a 2-inch discharge line, solves it.

    Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in Lawton

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain. We replace those pumps and controls to meet code. Two alternating pumps on one controller mean one can fail without flooding the pit. Some insurance carriers ask for test records.

    Backup Pump Options in Lawton, OK

    Storm-related power outages can stop sump pumps and well pumps, so a backup power plan is worth having. Outages tend to hit right when the rain is heaviest and the pit is filling fastest. The backup kicks on if the primary dies or the power goes out. Water-powered backups use city water pressure and need no battery, but they only make sense with strong municipal pressure and an approved backflow preventer on the line. They will not work on a well.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Lawton

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. A check valve that leaks lets the discharge column fall back into the pit, so the pump runs again for the same water and wears itself out early. Every pump we install gets a new check valve. In a tight pit we use a vertical switch, and in a wide pit a tethered float.

    Light Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Lawton, OK

    Light commercial work, think retail basements and small office buildings, usually means a 1/2 HP or larger cast iron pump with a 2-inch discharge and a panel alarm. We match the old pump or step it up. Grinder and ejector pumps are a separate job.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Lawton, OK

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They suit pits too tight for two separate pumps. Depending on what failed, we swap one pump or the entire unit.
