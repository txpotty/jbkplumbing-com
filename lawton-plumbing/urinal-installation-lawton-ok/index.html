    Rough-In Plumbing for Urinals in Lawton, OK

    Rough-in decides whether the finish goes smoothly. We set the carrier, run a 2 inch waste line to the outlet height on the spec sheet, stub the 3/4 inch supply where the flush valve will land, and tie the vent in before anyone hangs drywall. Inspectors see it open. Then the walls close.

    Waterless Urinal Upgrades in Lawton

    Switching to waterless is mostly a fixture swap. We remove the flush valve, cap the supply and set the waterless bowl on the same carrier. Many jurisdictions want a capped supply kept for later. That allows a future change back.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Lawton

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. That means a rim no higher than 17 inches above the floor, an elongated bowl projecting at least 13-1/2 inches from the wall, and a 30 by 48 inch clear floor space in front. Flush controls must be reachable. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Lawton, OK

    Carrier work is the biggest cost swing. Reusing a carrier and lines is cheap, while new carriers and pipe runs through finished walls cost more. Sensor valves add a few hundred. We itemize each piece.

    Office and Restaurant Urinal Installation in Lawton

    Restaurant restrooms see a lot of traffic in short bursts. Busy bars can see hundreds of flushes a night, so we install valves that recover fast and carriers that stay tight. Vandal-resistant valves are available. Night installs keep you open.

    Commercial Urinal Installation in Lawton

    Offices and restaurants want urinals that survive heavy use. Each unit goes on a carrier with new gaskets and stainless bolts, paired with a matched Sloan or Zurn valve. We check partition spacing too. Most codes want 15 inches from centerline to the side.

    Waterless Cartridge Urinals in Lawton

    Each waterless brand uses its own cartridge. Falcon, Waterless Co, Kohler and Zurn units all trap odor differently, and the cartridge or sealing liquid has to be replaced after a set number of uses, which in a busy restroom can mean every few months. A replacement schedule comes with the install. Spare cartridges should stay on site.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Lawton, OK

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. Tankless flushometers deliver a set volume from a 3/4 inch line and recover instantly for the next user. The volume matches the fixture rating. Wrong settings stain bowls.
