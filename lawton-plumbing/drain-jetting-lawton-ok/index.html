    Grease Line Jetting in Lawton, OK

    Grease turns hard once it cools. The line between the sinks and the interceptor gets packed with cooled grease until floor sinks overflow. We often jet with hot water. Scheduled cleaning keeps floor sinks draining and the interceptor doing its job.

    Restaurant Grease Line Jetting in Lawton

    Floor sinks backing up during service shut a kitchen down. Cooled grease narrows the line to the interceptor until each pot wash floods the floor. Hot water jetting breaks it loose. Clean lines also help with health inspections.

    Galvanized and cast iron drains collect mineral scale. Rough scale catches hair and grease on every flush, so clogs return faster year after year. A scale nozzle helps. Before and after camera footage shows how much pipe wall is left.
