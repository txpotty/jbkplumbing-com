    Slab Leak Repiping in Lawton

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Soil movement can separate sewer lines and water lines over time, so foundation shifts and plumbing problems often go together. Instead of breaking concrete to patch one pipe, we reroute new supply lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, then abandon the old lines in the slab. No jackhammer needed. That stops the current leak and removes the chance of the next one under the same floor.

    PEX Repiping in Lawton, OK

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. It resists scale and chlorine, handles a freeze better than copper and costs less to install, which is why most of our repipes use it. We use PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe open. That keeps pressure up at the far fixtures.
