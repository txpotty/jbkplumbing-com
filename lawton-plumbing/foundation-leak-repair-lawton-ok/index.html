    Foundation Leak Detection in Lawton

    Accurate detection is the key step in foundation leak repair. We isolate hot and cold lines and pressure test them to confirm a leak exists. Then we use acoustic listening equipment and electronic line tracing to find the exact spot beneath the foundation. Thermal imaging helps verify hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.

    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Lawton, OK

    When the leak is in an accessible spot and the rest of the pipe is in good condition, direct repair is often the most economical choice. We cut a small opening in the floor, excavate to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The soil is compacted and the concrete is patched to match. Flooring can then be restored by your flooring contractor.

    Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in Lawton, OK

    In some cases, the most effective way to repair a foundation leak is by tunneling beneath the slab. This method lets our plumbers reach the damaged pipe directly without breaking through your home's interior flooring. JBK Plumbing is experienced in safe and efficient tunneling, minimizing disruption to your Lawton, OK home and delivering a complete repair of the damaged pipe section.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Lawton, OK

    Many older homes in Lawton, OK have copper water lines beneath their foundations, which can corrode over time due to soil conditions or water chemistry. Much of Oklahoma has moderately hard to very hard water, especially in western areas, which leaves scale in water heaters and fixtures. When these copper pipes develop pinhole leaks or cracks, they need specialized repair. Our technicians repair or replace sections of damaged copper piping, providing a durable seal that withstands pressure and ground movement and helps prevent future leaks.

    Slab Leak Detection & Repair in Lawton

    Slab leaks are notorious for being difficult to detect, often showing subtle signs like warm spots on the floor or a sudden jump in your water bill. JBK Plumbing uses acoustic and infrared technology to accurately locate these hidden leaks under your concrete foundation. Once found, we offer fitting repair solutions, whether it's a spot repair, re-routing, or pipe bursting, to restore your plumbing system in Lawton without extensive damage to your property.

    Basement Wall Sealing and Waterproofing in Lawton, OK

    For homes with basements in Lawton, OK, wall leaks can be a significant concern. We offer complete basement waterproofing services, including interior and exterior sealing. This may involve applying waterproof coatings, injecting cracks with polyurethane or epoxy, or setting up interior drainage systems. These measures prevent water from penetrating porous concrete and masonry, keeping your basement dry and healthy.

    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Lawton, OK

    Leaks in drain lines under the foundation can be harder to detect because they are not under constant pressure. We use camera inspection and hydrostatic testing to find cracks and separated joints. Repairs may involve tunneling, cutting the slab or pipe lining. We explain the trade-offs of each method.

    Exterior Foundation Leak Solutions in Lawton

    Many foundation leaks start from exterior factors like poor drainage, overflowing gutters, or saturated soil. We assess your home's exterior to identify these culprits. Our solutions include grading adjustments, French drain installation, and downspout extensions to move water away from your foundation. Proper exterior water management is crucial for preventing hydrostatic pressure buildup against your foundation walls in Lawton and keeping water out.
