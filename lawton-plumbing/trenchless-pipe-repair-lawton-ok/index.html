    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Lawton, OK

    A broken water service line wastes water and soaks the yard. It also drops pressure. After locating the leak, we burst new HDPE through the old path or bore a new one, keeping the lawn and walk intact. We pressure test before turning water on.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Lawton

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Lawton yard.

    Cost-Effective Underground Pipe Repair in Lawton, OK

    People are often surprised at the final number. It's often cheaper in the end. Per foot, lining can look pricey, but once you add the cost of tearing out and replacing a driveway, a patio and the landscaping above an open trench, the trenchless bid usually comes out ahead.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Lawton, OK

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. A winch pulls a bursting head through the line, cracking the old pipe outward into the soil while new HDPE follows right behind it, so the replacement pipe ends up in the same path. HDPE resists corrosion and leaks in Lawton, OK soil.
