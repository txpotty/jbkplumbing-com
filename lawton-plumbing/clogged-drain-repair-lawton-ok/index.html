    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Drain Repair in Lawton

    Clearing one sink, tub or shower drain usually costs $125 to $300. Hard clogs cost a bit more. A clog that needs a larger machine or access through a roof vent is typically $250 to $450, and jetting a heavily scaled or greasy line usually costs between $350 and $800. The price is set up front.

    Much of Oklahoma has moderately hard to very hard water, especially in western areas, which leaves mineral deposits in water heaters and fixtures. Scale narrows the pipe and gives grease and hair something to cling to. Old cast iron makes it worse. Older homes may have cast iron that rusts and roughens inside, so each bit of debris catches on the wall and the clog builds faster than it would in smooth plastic pipe.

    Most single fixture clogs take 30 to 90 minutes. A few run over. Lines with heavy buildup, poor access or a clog that has been treated with chemicals take more time because we have to work carefully. We tell you if it will run long.

    Clearing a clog does not need a permit. It is just maintenance. A permit may be needed if the fix turns into replacing pipe, digging up part of the sewer line or changing the layout of the drain system in the house. We tell you in advance if that applies in Lawton.

    Call when a plunger does not work. Odors are a sign too. Water backing up into other fixtures, or multiple slow drains usually mean a main line issue. Those need a bigger machine.

    Not if you can avoid it. They are harsh. Caustic products can corrode older pipes and soften plastic fittings, and when they fail to clear the clog they sit in the trap and make the repair riskier for everyone. A cable is safer and works better.

    Snaking uses a cable with a cutting or grabbing head. It opens a path. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scrub the pipe wall, which removes the grease, scale and roots that a cable leaves behind and often keeps a line clear far longer. We use both, depending on the pipe.
