    PEX Pipe Replacement in Lawton

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a common choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Freezes are less frequent in Oklahoma than in northern states, but hard cold snaps and ice storms do happen and can burst exposed pipes. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during sudden cold snaps. JBK Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials. This modern solution offers strong performance and water quality for your home.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Lawton

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack without warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when selling the home.

    PEX Repiping in Lawton

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for better flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with many fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most requested repipe material.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Lawton, OK

    Many older homes in Lawton, OK still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to rust and corrosion on the inside. This leads to reduced water pressure, discolored water, and eventual leaks. JBK Plumbing provides complete galvanized pipe replacement services, removing these failing pipes and installing new, modern materials. Upgrading from galvanized pipes not only improves your water quality and pressure but also protects your home from potential water damage caused by deteriorating plumbing.
