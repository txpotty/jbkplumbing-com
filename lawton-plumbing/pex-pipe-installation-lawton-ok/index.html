    Copper to PEX Conversions in Lawton

    Pinhole leaks in copper rarely show up alone. Aggressive water, fast flow and soil contact under slabs can pit copper from the inside, and the first few pinholes are typically followed by more. Many rural homes rely on private wells, and some groundwater carries high mineral content that benefits from softening or filtration. A switch to PEX ends it.     Uponor PEX System Installation in Lawton

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool and a cold-expansion ring. The tech expands the pipe and ring together, pushes the fitting in, and the plastic shrinks back around the barb over the next few seconds, tighter than when it started. There is no crimp gauge to check. Because the fittings are wide inside, each joint costs very little flow.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Lawton

    Low pressure at every faucet, not just one, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures.     PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Lawton

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A makes tighter bends, can be heat-fixed if it kinks, and its fittings flow better, while PEX-B is cheaper and its crimps hold just as well when each one is gauged. Either one lasts for decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.
