    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Lawton

    Storm-related power outages can stop sump pumps and well pumps, so a backup power plan is worth having. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Lawton, OK

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil around the slab or foundation.

    Emergency Sump Pump Maintenance in Lawton

    If your sump pump is acting up or has stopped working, JBK Plumbing provides emergency maintenance services in Lawton. We focus on rapid response to diagnose and address urgent issues, whether it's a jammed impeller, a faulty float switch or a complete pump failure. Our goal is to quickly restore your sump pump's function to prevent immediate flooding and protect your property from water damage.

    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Lawton

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last longer.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Lawton, OK

    If your sump pump is acting up, making unusual noises or not turning on, our emergency diagnostic service can quickly identify the problem.
