    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Owasso, OK

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. A battery backup pump sits beside the primary and runs on its own float. Most run five to seven hours on a full charge. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Owasso

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Owasso, OK

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. Their 250 and 280 series cover most residential lifts, and the vertical float version fits narrow pits where a wide tethered float would rub. Replacement switches are easy to get. A small weep hole in the discharge pipe keeps the pump from air locking.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Owasso, OK

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary runs on house power and handles the everyday inflow, while the battery unit waits above it on a separate float. People with carpet downstairs buy these. They are worth it anywhere a few inches of water would mean new drywall.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Owasso, OK

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Batteries need replacing every few years. They matter most below finished rooms.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Owasso

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume.     Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Owasso, OK

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. You can reach the motor and the switch without fishing anything out of the pit, which helps in a basement where the pit is shallow or tight. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.
