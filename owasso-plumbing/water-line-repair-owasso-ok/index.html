    PEX Water Line Repair in Owasso, OK

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rodents chew it.     Trenchless Water Line Repair in Owasso

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Owasso

We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, while PEX gets a fresh crimp ring or expansion fitting with the right tool for the brand.
