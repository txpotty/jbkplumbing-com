    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Owasso, OK

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested. We carry proven brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Owasso, OK

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Owasso, OK, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a popular and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Owasso

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Owasso

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a widely used backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Owasso, DCVAs are frequently installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers precisely install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Owasso

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are often required for high-hazard applications in Owasso. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. JBK Plumbing focuses on precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with trusted brands like Watts and Febco.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Owasso
