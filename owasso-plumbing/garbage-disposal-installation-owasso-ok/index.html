    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Owasso, OK

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Owasso, OK

    Air switches start the disposal with a button on the sink or counter. The button pushes air through a tube to a control box in the cabinet, which keeps electricity away from the button and avoids a new wall switch. They are common on islands.

    Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Owasso

    Batch-feed disposals only run when the stopper lid is in place, which adds safety for families with kids. They need no wall switch, so they are a good choice for kitchens where adding a switch would mean cutting into finished drywall or tile backsplash. Mounting and drains are standard.
