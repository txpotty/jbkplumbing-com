    Expansion Tank Sizing for Large Water Heaters in Owasso

    A standard 2 gallon tank works for many 40 and 50 gallon heaters at moderate pressure. Larger heaters, higher incoming pressure or high-capacity systems may need a 4.5 gallon tank or larger. We use manufacturer sizing charts that factor in heater volume, supply pressure and temperature setting. Correct sizing keeps the tank from over-flexing and wearing out early.

    Standard Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Owasso

    Our standard installation service covers traditional tank-style water heaters, with the expansion tank correctly tied into your cold water supply line.     New Expansion Tank Installation in Owasso

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve and no expansion tank, the water heater is likely seeing a pressure spike every heating cycle.     Pressure Reducing Valve and Expansion Tank Pairing in Owasso

    Many homes see high incoming pressure, so pressure reducing valves are widespread. The PRV protects your fixtures but also turns your plumbing into a closed system, which makes an expansion tank necessary. We test the PRV output and replace it if it is creeping or failing. Pairing a healthy PRV with a correctly charged expansion tank gives you steady pressure throughout the house.
