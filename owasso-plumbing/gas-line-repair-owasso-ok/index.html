    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Owasso

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. Leaking joints get taken apart, cleaned, resealed with gas-rated compound and tightened, and damaged fittings or valves get replaced. Every repair gets a soap test and a system pressure test before we leave.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Owasso, OK

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Years of vibration from a furnace or a house settling can loosen threaded fittings, and pipe in a damp garage or an exterior wall can rust from the outside in. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.

    CSST Repair and Bonding in Owasso, OK

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. CSST can be damaged by nails, rodents or lightning, and older yellow-jacket CSST needs proper bonding to the electrical grounding system to reduce the risk of a lightning arc. It takes about an hour. Damaged runs get spliced with the manufacturer's fittings, and we confirm the bond on the way out. Storm-related power outages can stop sump pumps and well pumps, so a standby power plan is worth having.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Owasso

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. With every appliance off, a moving test dial on the meter tells us gas is escaping somewhere, and then we isolate branches to narrow it down. A combustible gas detector narrows it further. Soap solution at the suspect fitting confirms it with bubbles, and then we repair the joint, replace the section or swap the connector before testing again.
