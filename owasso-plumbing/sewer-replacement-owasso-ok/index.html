    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Owasso, OK

    Trenching lets us fix grade. That is its big advantage. When an old line has a sag that holds water and solids, bursting would just follow the same sag, while a trench lets us lay new pipe at the right pitch. Bellies disappear.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Owasso, OK

    Pipe bursting needs two pits. One sits at the house, the other at the far end of the run. A cone-shaped head is pulled through the old line, breaking it outward, while fused HDPE pipe follows right behind it, which puts new pipe in the same spot without digging the yard. Driveways stay put.
