    Picking a Tankless for Your Broken Arrow, OK House

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Broken Arrow

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. The gas line usually has to be 3/4-inch or larger. Pipe length and meter capacity get checked against the sizing tables, and we confirm pressure at full fire. An undersized line causes ignition faults.

    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Broken Arrow

    No hot water from a tankless usually shows up as an error code on the controller, and the code tells us half of what we need to know. Send us a photo of the display. A leaking heat exchanger means replacement, usually done same day or next day.

    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in Broken Arrow, OK

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Over 20 years it can come out close, since a tankless can outlast two tanks and skips standby loss, but the payback depends on how much hot water your house uses. We price both.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Broken Arrow, OK

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. Static pressure means little if it drops when the burner opens up. Both readings get taken.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Broken Arrow

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. Newer Rinnai models usually fit the old mounting pattern. Less drywall work that way. Venting stays the same, or switches to PVC if you move to a condensing unit.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Broken Arrow, OK

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Their combi boilers handle both space heat and domestic water. We set up both.

    Leaking Tankless Replacement in Broken Arrow

    A leaking tankless can drip inside the cabinet for weeks before anyone notices the puddle. Close the cold isolation valve and the gas cock first. Then we look at whether the leak is a fitting or the heat exchanger itself, because one is a $20 fix and the other means a new unit.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Broken Arrow, OK

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Plenty of panels do not have room. We check panel capacity and wire gauge before quoting.
