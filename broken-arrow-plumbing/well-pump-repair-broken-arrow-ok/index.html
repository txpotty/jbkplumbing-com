    Well Pump Diagnostic Services in Broken Arrow, OK

    Voltage, amperage, pressure and flow rate tell us most of what is happening down the well without pulling a single length of pipe. A weak motor draws high amps.     Deep wells get submersible pumps. They push water up, run quietly and last, but motors, impellers and splices still wear out.     Control Box and Surge Repair in Broken Arrow, OK

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Severe thunderstorms and heavy spring rain can cause flash flooding in Oklahoma, overwhelming drainage and backing up sewers. Surges from lightning are the usual cause of control box failures. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection, replace what failed and recommend a surge arrestor at the box if one is missing. Arrestors are cheap.

    Above-Ground Jet Pump Repair in Broken Arrow

    Lost prime is the number one jet pump complaint. Shaft seal leaks and worn impellers come next. We reprime the pump, replace the seal and check the foot valve and suction line for leaks, since one tiny air leak will undo the whole repair. Everything is above ground.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Broken Arrow
