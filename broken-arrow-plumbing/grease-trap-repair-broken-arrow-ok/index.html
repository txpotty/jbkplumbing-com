    Commercial Grease Trap Repair in Broken Arrow

    Restaurants, school cafeterias, hospital kitchens and bakeries all push more grease than a small trap can hold. We see undersized traps fail in the same few places every time.     Preventative Grease Trap Maintenance & Repair in Broken Arrow, OK

    Most trap repairs we do trace back to pumping intervals that stretched too long. The common rule is to pump when grease and solids fill a quarter of the liquid depth. After a repair, we measure the grease cap and solids with a sludge judge and suggest a pumping schedule based on how fast your trap actually fills. A kitchen running two fryers can hit that mark in a couple of weeks.

    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Broken Arrow

    Hardened grease in the inlet tee is the usual culprit when a trap backs up. We pump the trap down, then jet the inlet line with a 4,000 PSI unit until water runs free. Solids packed against the baffle come out by hand, with a scoop and a bucket. Then we look for the reason it clogged, such as a dishwasher dumping too much hot water at once or a missing sink strainer upstream. A clog that keeps coming back nearly always has a cause you can see.

    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in Broken Arrow, OK

    A wet floor around an under-sink trap usually points to a failed lid gasket or a loose inlet coupling. Older steel bodies rust out along the bottom seams, too. We dry the area, fill the trap and watch where the water shows up. Some days the fix is a cheap gasket and ten minutes. An opened seam is another story, and we will say up front if a patch will not hold.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Broken Arrow, OK

    Lines downstream of the trap should stay clean if the trap is working. When we find grease past the outlet, the trap is not doing its job. We jet the downstream run to the property line or the sewer tap, then camera it to confirm the pipe is clear and to document any cracked or sagging sections for a repair quote. If we only clear the line and skip the trap, it clogs again.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Broken Arrow

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. Once one cracks or falls loose, the trap is just a box that grease passes through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. On plastic units we order the factory part for that model number. Steel traps with a rusted-out baffle may need a fabricated piece, and sometimes a new trap is the cheaper answer.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Broken Arrow, OK

    Tees are cheap parts with an outsized job. The inlet tee sends flow down below the grease layer. The outlet tee draws water from low in the tank, well under the grease cap, so if it breaks off or someone removes it during pumping, floating grease walks right out to the sewer. New tees go in as Schedule 40 PVC, cut to the drop length the manufacturer or local code specifies.

    Commercial Grease Interceptor Repair in Broken Arrow, OK

    In-ground gravity interceptors run from about 500 gallons to several thousand, usually precast concrete with two compartments. Over the years, hydrogen sulfide gas softens the concrete above the water line. We pump the tank, inspect the walls and the crossover, and repair spalled concrete with a cementitious patch or an epoxy liner rated for that service. Nobody goes in without a gas monitor, a tripod and a second person topside.
