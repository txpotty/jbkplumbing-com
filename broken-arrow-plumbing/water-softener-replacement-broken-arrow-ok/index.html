    Whole-Home Softener Placement in Broken Arrow, OK

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Broken Arrow

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. We replace timer units with metered valves that regenerate on gallons used. You will buy less salt. The brine setting gets matched to your hardness so the unit is not wasting salt with every cycle.

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Broken Arrow, OK

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. Hardness gets tested on both sides of the unit, and the cheap fixes get tried before we talk replacement. Obsolete valves are another story.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Broken Arrow

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico runs them without electricity, using water flow to drive the valve. They cost more.

    Softener Loop Installation in Broken Arrow, OK

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. Homes without one get a loop cut in, with shutoffs and a bypass. Hose bibs stay off it. The drain goes to a laundry box or floor drain with an air gap.
