    Foundation Crack Repair in Broken Arrow

    Foundation cracks are a common entry point for basement water leaks, especially in homes built on soil that swells and shrinks. Most homes sit on concrete slabs, so leaks under the floor often call for slab leak detection before repair. We utilize epoxy and polyurethane injection techniques to seal these cracks, preventing water infiltration and reinforcing your foundation. Our methods are tailored to the specific type and size of the crack, ensuring a durable repair.

    Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in Broken Arrow, OK

    Cast iron drain stacks in older homes crack and scale with age, and plastic joints can separate if a line was poorly supported. These leaks often show up only when an upstairs fixture runs. We run water through each drain while watching the basement, then verify the crack with a camera. Repairs range from a single section swap to replacing an entire stack with PVC or ABS.

    Wall and Slab Seepage Repair in Broken Arrow

    When water enters through foundation walls or the slab joint, the cause is most often surface water pooling against the house. Most homes sit on concrete slabs, so leaks under the floor often call for professional leak detection before repair. Dense or saturated soil can hold water near the foundation instead of letting it drain away. We seal penetrations where pipes pass through the wall and can install interior drain channels that carry seepage to a sump. We also recommend grading and gutter changes when outside water is the root cause.

    Clogged Drain Tile Repair in Broken Arrow

    Interior or exterior drain tile systems can become clogged with sediment or tree roots over time, leaving them useless. We specialize in diagnosing and clearing blockages in these critical drainage systems, restoring their ability to divert water away from your basement walls and foundation in Broken Arrow.

    Interior Drainage Systems in Broken Arrow

    When exterior excavation isn't practical, interior drainage systems provide an effective solution for managing basement leaks. We install perimeter drains along the basement walls, collecting water before it can surface and directing it to a sump pump for removal. This system is less disruptive and can be a cost-effective way to dry out a wet basement.

    Exterior Waterproofing in Broken Arrow, OK

    For a more complete solution, we offer exterior waterproofing, which involves excavating around your foundation, applying a waterproof membrane, and installing drainage boards.     Floor Drain and Backwater Valve Repair in Broken Arrow

    A basement floor drain that backs up during heavy use or a storm usually points to a blockage or a failed trap.
