    Leak Detection and Assessment in Broken Arrow, OK

    Hidden leaks waste water and quietly damage floors and walls. Start at the meter. With all fixtures off, we watch the meter's leak indicator, then use acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera to trace the leak behind walls, under floors or in the slab. Early detection limits the damage. Most homes sit on concrete slabs, so leaks under the floor often call for slab leak detection before repair.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Broken Arrow

    Many rural homes rely on private wells, and some groundwater carries high mineral content that calls for softening or filtration. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. Then we explain your options. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.
