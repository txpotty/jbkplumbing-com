    Shower Drain Body Repair in Broken Arrow

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. When that happens, water goes under the shower instead of down the pipe, and the first sign is often a soft spot in the floor next to the shower or a stain in the ceiling below. We clear and test the drain. Loose drains get resealed, and cracked ones get replaced.

    Emergency Shower Repair in Broken Arrow, OK

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Close the main valve where the water line enters the house, or the shower's own stops if it has them behind an access panel. Then call us. JBK Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Broken Arrow

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water running down the inside of the trim gets behind the tile at the valve opening, and over a few months it soaks the drywall around the valve without ever showing on the shower side. The trim comes off first. Worn O-rings or a loose bonnet nut are the usual cause.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Broken Arrow

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. In a single-handle shower the cause is almost always the cartridge or its O-rings, and on an old two-handle valve it is the stem washer and the seat. The parts are on the truck. Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister cartridges cover most of the valves we open, so the repair is usually done that same visit.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Broken Arrow

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. We fix the valve. Drips from the arm threads just need fresh tape and a snug turn.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Broken Arrow, OK

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. We pull the strainer and run a hand auger through the trap, and if the clog sits past the trap in the branch line, we switch to a drum machine with a smaller cable. Drain cleaner usually misses the clog. We recommend a hair catcher once it is clear.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Broken Arrow, OK

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. The Moen 1222 runs the Posi-Temp line, and the Delta RP46074 runs the Monitor 13 and 14 series, and both wear out on a schedule you can almost set a calendar by. We stock both. Original owners can often get the cartridge free under warranty.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Broken Arrow

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Scale builds behind the face plate and in the flow restrictor, so the spray goes uneven, shoots sideways or drops to a trickle even when the valve is wide open. A vinegar soak can work. Otherwise we put on a new head with rubber nozzles that rub clean with your thumb.
