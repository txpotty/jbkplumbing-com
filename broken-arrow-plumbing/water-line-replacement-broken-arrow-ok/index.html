    PEX Water Line Installation in Broken Arrow, OK

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Broken Arrow, OK

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Copper and poly are better pulled than burst.     Type L vs. Type K Copper in Broken Arrow, OK

    The thickest wall belongs to Type K, the usual choice underground. Type L is lighter and mostly goes indoors. Some codes allow L for burial, but we stick with K for service lines because a thicker wall buys more years against corrosive soil and stray rocks. Same fittings, different pipe.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Broken Arrow

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. It does not corrode or build scale. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, connect it with brass or poly fittings that are approved underground, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector.
