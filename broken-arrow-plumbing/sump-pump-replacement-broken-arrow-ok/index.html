    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Broken Arrow

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Broken Arrow for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, properly sized pump. We make sure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and maximize efficiency. That is key for handling heavy rainfall.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Broken Arrow

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Sump Pump Basin and Pit Replacement in Broken Arrow, OK

    Sometimes it is not just the pump that needs replacing, since the sump basin or pit itself may be cracked, damaged or too small. JBK Plumbing offers sump pump basin and pit replacement in Broken Arrow, OK so your entire system works properly. A properly sized and intact pit is essential for the efficient operation and longevity of your new sump pump.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Broken Arrow

    Power outages during severe storms can leave a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump offers important protection during these times. We install and replace complete battery backup systems, including dedicated marine-grade batteries and smart charging units.     Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Broken Arrow

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins. We replace ejector pumps with models rated for two inch solids. We also check the basin seal, vent and discharge check valve. Proper installation stops sewer gas odors.
