    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Shawnee

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. We time the cycles and measure how fast the pit refills. The rest is simple arithmetic. Often a 1/2 or 3/4 HP pump with more GPM at your lift, plus a 2-inch discharge line, solves it.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Shawnee, OK

    A pit that is filling with the pump silent means you have maybe an hour or two before water crosses the basement floor, depending on how fast it is coming in. Check the breaker and the GFCI outlet first, then lift the lid and see whether the float is stuck. A shop vac or a bucket can hold the level down. Our trucks carry common sizes, so most emergency calls end with a new pump running the same visit.

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Shawnee

    Cast iron sheds motor heat into the water around it far better than a plastic housing does, which is a big part of why a Zoeller M53 or Liberty 250 outlasts a hardware-store pump. Figure on an hour or two for a typical swap. We also check that the float has room to travel without touching the basin wall.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Shawnee

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. When the check valve leaks, the water in the pipe drops back into the basin and the pump has to move it twice, which shortens its life. New valve every time. In a tight pit we use a vertical switch, and in a wide pit a tethered float.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Shawnee

    Basement bathrooms below the sewer line drain into a sealed, vented basin, and the ejector pump in that basin has to pass 2-inch solids. That is not a sump pump job. We replace the ejector and its discharge check valve, reseal the lid gasket, and confirm the vent ties in. A bad seal shows up as sewer odor.

    Commercial Sump Pump Service in Shawnee

    Commercial sumps run harder than residential ones, and a restaurant or office basement often has a duplex system with two pumps that alternate on a controller. Both pumps and the alternator get swapped together. Work can be scheduled early or after closing so nobody is stepping around an open basin.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Shawnee

    Storm-related power outages can stop sump pumps and well pumps, so a standby power plan is worth having. A primary pump on household current does nothing once the breaker panel goes dark. We add a 12-volt backup pump on its own float, mounted a few inches above the primary, with a deep-cycle battery and a charger that tests itself. It also covers a stuck primary switch. The alarm will tell you which one is running.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Shawnee, OK

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. An existing Zoeller can usually be swapped for a current model with the same 1.5-inch outlet and float style. Need more lift? We can move you up to a half-horse model like the M98.
