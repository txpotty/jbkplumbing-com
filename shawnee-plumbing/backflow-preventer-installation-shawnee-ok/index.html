    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Shawnee, OK

    Pressure vacuum breakers are the low-cost answer for lawn irrigation in Shawnee, OK. They let air in when supply pressure drops, which breaks the siphon, but they only protect against back-siphonage, so they must sit above every downstream outlet and cannot be used where back pressure is possible. We check clearance and drainage too.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Shawnee

    Two independent checks and a relief valve make up an RPZ. If the zone between the checks loses its pressure margin, the relief valve opens, dumping water to the floor or a drain so nothing can pass upstream, which is why we never bury one in a pit in Shawnee. We install Watts and Febco units.

    Commercial Backflow Prevention Systems in Shawnee, OK

    Commercial buildings in Shawnee, OK usually need containment at the meter and isolation devices inside. Our plumber walks the building and lists each cross-connection, from the boiler makeup line to the dish machine, then install the assemblies the water provider calls for and schedule the work around your business hours. Weekend installs are available.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Shawnee, OK

    High-hazard connections in Shawnee, OK call for a reduced pressure zone assembly. Think chemical injection or medical equipment, where a backflow event could put something toxic in the supply, so the RPZ adds a relief valve between two checks that dumps water if either check leaks. It has to drain somewhere.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Shawnee

    A double check valve assembly, or DCVA, uses two spring-loaded checks in series with four test cocks. It is the usual pick for low-hazard lines such as many fire sprinklers, and because it does not discharge water, it can go in a vault where local rules allow it, sized so it does not rob your system of pressure in Shawnee. We leave room for the tester.
