    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Shawnee, OK

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Shawnee, OK

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Many homes with crawlspaces or deep window wells use small pumps to clear runoff. We service these smaller units, clear their screens and confirm the discharge reaches daylight or an approved drain. Before a cold front, homeowners around Shawnee should disconnect hoses, cover outdoor spigots and let faucets drip overnight.

    Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in Shawnee

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. We test duplex systems, alternating controls and high-water alarms on a scheduled basis. Maintenance logs help property managers stay on top of inspections.

    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Shawnee

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last longer.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Shawnee, OK

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil near the slab or foundation.

    Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in Shawnee

    Regular annual maintenance is key for the longevity and reliability of your sump pump. Our technicians perform a thorough inspection, checking the motor, impeller, float switch and discharge lines for any signs of wear or blockage. We also test the system's operation by filling the pit with water to ensure it activates correctly and pumps water away efficiently, protecting your Shawnee home from unexpected flooding.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Shawnee

    Our team is skilled in maintaining submersible sump pumps, a popular choice in Shawnee due to their quiet operation and compact design. Maintenance involves pulling the pump to inspect the watertight seals, clearing the intake screen and checking the internal components for corrosion or wear. We also verify the electrical connections are secure and free from moisture, preventing electrical hazards and supporting longevity.
