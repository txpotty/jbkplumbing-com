    Control Valve Repair in Shawnee

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, directing the flow of water and starting regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water or waste salt. JBK Plumbing technicians are experts at diagnosing and repairing common control valve issues, whether mechanical or electronic, ensuring precise timing and efficient operation. We stock common replacement parts to speed your repair in Shawnee.

    Hard Water Symptoms & Diagnostics in Shawnee

    If you're noticing scale buildup on faucets, cloudy dishes, dry skin and hair, or clothes that feel stiff after washing, your water softener likely needs attention. JBK Plumbing offers complete diagnostic services to identify the root cause of these hard water symptoms. We check water hardness levels, inspect all system components, and provide an accurate assessment and repair plan suited to your home in Shawnee.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Shawnee, OK

    A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms in the brine tank and keeps salt from dissolving. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. We also recommend salt types that bridge less in warm or humid spaces.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Shawnee, OK

    Leaks in a water softener can range from minor drips around fittings to significant puddles from a cracked tank or faulty valve. These leaks can cause water damage and waste water. Our plumbers promptly pinpoint the source of leaks, whether from seals, O-rings, hoses, or internal components, and perform lasting repairs. We use appropriate sealing compounds and replacement parts for a lasting fix in Shawnee, OK.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Shawnee

    Over time, the resin beads in your water softener can become fouled or degrade, losing their ability to exchange ions and soften water. This is a frequent issue, especially with older systems or those exposed to high levels of chlorine. JBK Plumbing provides professional resin bed replacement services, carefully removing old resin and installing new, high-quality media to restore your softener's efficiency and capacity in Shawnee.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Shawnee, OK

    While less common than traditional softeners, no-salt water conditioners also require maintenance and periodic repair. These systems work by conditioning water to prevent scale buildup without removing minerals. If you notice scale returning or reduced water pressure, your no-salt system may need attention. JBK Plumbing is experienced in diagnosing and repairing issues with these specialized units in Shawnee, OK, ensuring they continue to protect your plumbing and appliances effectively.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Shawnee

    A frequent issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to inefficient regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and making sure the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering precise repairs or replacements.
