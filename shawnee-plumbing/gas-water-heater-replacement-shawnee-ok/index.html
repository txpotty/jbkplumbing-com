    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Replacement in Shawnee

    The cost for gas water heater replacement in Shawnee, OK typically ranges from $1,200 to $3,000, including the unit and installation. This can vary based on the model, capacity and any necessary changes to gas lines or venting. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your needs.

    Most new gas water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty, usually covering the tank for 6 to 10 years and parts for 1 to 2 years. JBK Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation for extra peace of mind.

    Most standard gas water heater replacements in Shawnee can be completed within 3 to 5 hours. More complex installations, such as converting to a different type of unit or significant plumbing changes, may take longer.

    Atmospheric vent water heaters use natural convection to vent combustion gases up a chimney or flue. Power vent models use an electric fan to force gases out, allowing for more flexible venting options and often higher efficiency due to more complete combustion.

    Most cities and counties in Oklahoma require a permit for water heater replacement because it involves gas and venting. We handle the permit when one is required.

    Tank warranties typically range from 6 to 12 years depending on the model. We add our own labor warranty on the installation.

    Most gas tanks last 8 to 12 years. Sediment from hard water and extreme garage temperatures can cut that. Annual flushing and anode checks help them last longer.
