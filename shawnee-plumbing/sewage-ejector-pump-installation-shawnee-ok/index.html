    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Shawnee

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin usually costs $900 to $2,000. A new ejector system with basin, venting and floor work usually ranges from $2,500 to $6,000 or more.

    JBK Plumbing offers a comprehensive warranty on all sewage ejector pump installations, covering both parts and labor. The warranty period can vary based on the pump manufacturer's warranty and our service guarantee. We will provide full details on your coverage before any work begins.

    We recommend having your sewage ejector pump inspected annually, especially if it is in a high-use area. Regular inspections can identify potential issues early, extend the life of your pump and prevent unexpected failures and costly backups. This is especially important for older units.

    Most ejector pumps last 7 to 10 years with normal use. Flushing wipes or other non-flushable items can shorten that life significantly.

    Avoid flushing wipes, paper towels, feminine products, grease and cat litter. These items clog pumps and cause premature failure.

    Common signs that you might need a new sewage ejector pump include the pump running constantly, not turning on at all, making unusual grinding or loud noises, or foul odors or sewage backing up into your basement fixtures. These symptoms point to a failing unit that needs immediate attention.

    We install Zoeller, Liberty Pumps and Wayne ejector pumps, plus Saniflo macerating systems. We choose the brand and model based on your fixtures and lift height.

    A sewage ejector pump is designed to handle solid waste and wastewater from plumbing fixtures below the main sewer line, like toilets and sinks, discharging it into the sewer system. A sump pump, in contrast, removes groundwater from a basement or crawl space, only handling clear water to prevent flooding. They serve distinct purposes for different types of water.
