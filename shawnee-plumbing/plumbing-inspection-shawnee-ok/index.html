    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Shawnee, OK

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. The visit covers flushing the heater, working the shutoffs, reading pressure and checking for slow drains and small leaks. It takes about an hour. Doing this every year or two adds years to a water heater and keeps the shutoffs working for the day you really need them.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Shawnee

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked. We also look at the expansion tank, drain pan and softener, and a hardness test shows whether the softener is working or just eating salt.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Shawnee, OK

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.
