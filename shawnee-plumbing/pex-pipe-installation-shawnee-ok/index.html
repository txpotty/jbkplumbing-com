    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Shawnee

    The cost of PEX pipe installation in Shawnee, OK varies with the scope of the project, such as a full repipe or a small repair. In general, homeowners can expect to pay between $3,000 and $10,000 for a whole-home repipe, while smaller projects might range from $500 to $2,000. We provide detailed, clear estimates before starting any work.

    PEX resists scale and corrosion, flexes with temperature changes and has fewer joints. Copper is very durable but prone to pinhole leaks in some water conditions and costs more to install.

    We make small, targeted openings to run new pipe and patch them when done. Most homeowners are surprised by how little drywall work is needed.

    We install quality PEX piping from reputable manufacturers such as Uponor (PEX-A), Viega (PEX-B) and Zurn (PEX-B). We also work with other proven brands like SharkBite for fittings, so the best materials are used for your installation.

    Properly installed PEX is expected to last several decades. Manufacturers often offer 25 year warranties when their pipe and fittings are used together.

    We install Uponor PEX-A along with Viega, Zurn and SharkBite PEX-B systems. We recommend the system that fits your project and budget.

    PEX can be used for outdoor applications like irrigation lines or outdoor spigots, but it needs to be protected from direct UV light, which can degrade the material over time. When used outdoors, PEX should consistently be buried or otherwise shielded.
