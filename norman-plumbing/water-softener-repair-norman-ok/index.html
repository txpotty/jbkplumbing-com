    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Norman, OK

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. The bridge gets broken up and the tank cleaned out. Older brine tanks can crack, and we repair or replace those too. Keep the salt about half full.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Norman

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units regenerate on a schedule no matter how much water you used. We retest hardness and reprogram the valve, or upgrade the head to a metered control that only regenerates when the resin is actually close to exhausted. Salt bills drop.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Norman

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush. We test resin capacity, then clean it with resin cleaner or replace it, and a solid tank and valve with fresh resin works like new for years. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Water Softener Leak Detection & Repair in Norman, OK

    Control Valve Repair in Norman

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Norman

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. Salt sits there unused, and regeneration fails. We break the bridge, clean out the mushy salt and check the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.
