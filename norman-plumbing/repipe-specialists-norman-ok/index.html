    Efficient PEX Repiping in Norman, OK

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very popular choice for repiping projects in Norman, OK. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also highly resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use high-quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a dependable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Norman

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Most homes sit on concrete slabs, so leaks under the floor often call for professional leak detection before repair. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.
