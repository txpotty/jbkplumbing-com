    Gas Shut Off by the Utility in Norman

    When your utility shuts off gas for a leak, you may be without hot water, heat or cooking until a licensed plumber fixes it.     Propane Gas Line Service in Norman, OK

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. A natural gas burner running on propane will burn rich and soot up. We test the system the same way we test natural gas piping.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Norman, OK

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. Many tankless heaters are rated at 150,000 to 199,000 BTU, which can be more than double what an old tank heater used, so the line feeding it often has to be upsized back toward the meter. We calculate the full load first. The new pipe gets sized so every burner in the house still has full pressure at once.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Norman, OK

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. A pool heater can pull 250,000 BTU or more, which can rob gas from the furnace and water heater if the line was sized for a grill. Sizing is the first thing we check. New and repaired lines get tested, buried to the required depth and laid with tracer wire.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Norman

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. We replace them with new coated stainless connectors sized for the appliance and add an accessible shutoff valve if the old one is missing or seized. Usually under an hour per appliance. Furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers each need a sound connection, and most need a drip leg before the appliance valve.

    CSST Repair and Bonding in Norman, OK

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. CSST can be damaged by nails, rodents or lightning, and older yellow-jacket CSST needs proper bonding to the electrical grounding system to reduce the risk of a lightning arc. Bonding is a short job. Damaged runs get spliced with the manufacturer's fittings, and we confirm the bond on the way out. Severe thunderstorms and heavy spring rain can cause flash flooding in Oklahoma, overwhelming drainage and backing up sewers.

    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Norman, OK

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it. Older uncoated brass connectors can crack at the flare nut, and they are no longer accepted by most codes or utilities. We replace them with coated stainless connectors. We also add a reachable shutoff and a drip leg where needed, and confirm the new connector is rated for that appliance's BTU input.
