    Backflow preventer testing in Norman, OK typically costs between $75 and $150. This fee covers the technician's visit, the test itself, and the submission of required reports to your local water authority. Complex commercial systems or multiple devices may carry higher costs.

    JBK Plumbing stands behind our work with a comprehensive warranty on all backflow preventer repairs and installations. We use quality parts and hold our workmanship to the highest standards. Warranty details will be provided with your service agreement.

    An RPZ, or Reduced Pressure Zone assembly, offers the highest level of protection, suitable for high-hazard cross-connections. A DCVA, or Double Check Valve Assembly, provides moderate protection and is typically used for low to medium hazard situations, like irrigation systems.     We service all major brands of backflow preventers, including Watts, Wilkins, Febco, Conbraco and Apollo, and Zurn. Our technicians are trained and equipped to handle various models and types, so your device receives expert attention and the proper parts for repair.

    Signs of a failing backflow preventer include discolored water, unusual tastes or odors in your water, water leaking from the relief valve, or a sudden drop in water pressure. If you notice any of these issues, it is important to have your device inspected right away.

    Backflow prevention is crucial because of changing water pressures, main breaks and fire flow that can drop pressure suddenly, and the potential for cross-connections from irrigation systems or industrial processes.
