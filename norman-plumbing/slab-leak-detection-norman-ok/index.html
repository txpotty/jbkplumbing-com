    How We Find Slab Leaks in Norman, OK

    Tracer Gas Leak Detection in Norman

    PEX and CPVC do not carry sound well. For those, we drain the line and push in a safe hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas, which rises through the slab and gets picked up by a sniffer at the floor surface, even through grout lines and small cracks. It finds leaks acoustic gear misses in Norman homes.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Norman

    Hot lines leak under slabs more often than cold ones. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, wearing against the gravel and concrete until a pinhole opens. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Gas or electric bills may jump too.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Norman

    A reroute abandons the leaking line under the floor. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, which avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than opening a finished floor. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. It suits tile and hardwood homes.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Norman

    Water flooding a hallway or seeping through grout needs fast attention. Shut the main. We respond quickly, help you isolate the leaking side and often restore water to the rest of the house the same day, then come back to locate and repair the line properly.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Norman, OK

    A leaking hot line heats the concrete over it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. It only works on hot lines. It shrinks the search area before we listen.

    Camera Inspection for Under-Slab Drains in Norman, OK

    Under-slab drains crack as well. A broken cast iron or PVC drain will not move the water meter, but it can cause odors, soggy floors and slow drains, which is why we push a camera through to look for cracks, bellies and pulled joints. You see the footage yourself before any repair talk starts in your Norman home.

    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Norman

    Pressure isolation is always the first step. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which side loses pressure over a set time. That tells us where to look. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Norman, OK home.
