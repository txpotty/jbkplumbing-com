    A single clogged sink or tub in Norman, OK usually runs $200 to $350. Root-filled mains and jobs that need jetting usually fall in the $400 to $1,000 range. Access matters too.     No permit is required for routine rooting. A new cleanout or a pipe repair may need one. Permits for repair work are on us.

    Most single-drain clogs take 1 to 3 hours from arrival to flow test. Main lines with heavy roots, or jobs that need a jetting pass, can stretch to half a day or more. Access is the wild card. With no usable cleanout, we pull a toilet to get in, and that adds about an hour.

    Rooting uses a spinning cable with cutting blades to break up clogs and roots. Jetting works another way. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scrub the pipe walls clean. Tough lines often get both, rooting to open the path and jetting to clean out what remains.

    Sewer pipes leak a little water vapor at the joints. Roots follow that moisture to the gap and push in. Once in, they grow quickly. In Norman, clay pipe with a joint every 2 to 3 feet and older cast iron with oakum joints are the most common targets.

    Rental machines are easy to get, but they are dangerous in untrained hands. Cables can kink, whip and wrap around a wrist in a second. They also get stuck. Getting a rental cable stuck in clay pipe can mean digging it out, which costs far more than a rooter visit.
