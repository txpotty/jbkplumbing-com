    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Norman, OK

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Norman

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Much of Oklahoma has moderately hard to very hard water, especially in western areas, which leaves mineral deposits in water heaters and fixtures. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more usually follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and lowers the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Norman, OK

    Expanding your home or remodeling a kitchen or bathroom often requires new plumbing lines. PEX is an excellent choice for these projects in Norman, OK due to its adaptability and ease of installation in existing wall cavities or new structures. We can seamlessly tie new PEX lines into your current system, ensuring proper water supply to new fixtures and appliances without compromising your existing plumbing.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Norman

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our plumbers in Norman can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.
