    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Norman

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Oklahoma enforces a statewide plumbing code based on a national model code, and plumbers must be licensed by the state. A small expansion tank on the cold line absorbs that extra volume, which keeps the relief valve from weeping and saves wear on faucets and fittings. We set the air charge to match your water pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Norman, OK

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Gas tankless models usually need a bigger gas line than the old tank had, plus a dedicated vent run through the wall or roof. They cost more to install. We confirm your meter and line can handle it first.
