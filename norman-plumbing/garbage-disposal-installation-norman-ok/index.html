    Disposal Upgrades in Norman, OK

    Upgrading from an old 1/3 HP unit to something quieter is a common request.     Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Norman

    New disposals come with a knockout plug in the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp. A plug left in backs up the dishwasher. An air gap or high loop goes in too, which keeps sink water from running back into the dishwasher.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Norman, OK

    An air button on the sink deck or counter turns the disposal on. Pressing the button sends a pulse of air through a small tube to a control box under the sink, so there is no electricity at the button and no need to drill for a wall switch. Islands use them often.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Norman

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit is bigger and heavier than a starter model, so we measure the cabinet first. Taller units can push the discharge outlet below the wall stub, and when that happens we either lower the drain at the wall or pick a model with a higher outlet so water does not sit in the chamber. We also check the circuit. Bigger motors draw more at startup.

    Replacing an Old Disposal in Norman, OK

    The old unit usually drops free with a turn of the mount ring, once the power is off and the dishwasher hose and discharge tube are disconnected. If the new unit is the same brand and mount style, the swap is faster, but we still replace the flange gasket and putty when the old seal has hardened. Seals harden with age. We haul the old unit away.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Installation in Norman, OK

    Batch-feed units only run with the lid in place, and the lid holds a magnet that closes a switch inside the unit. No wall switch is needed, which makes them handy in kitchens where running a new switch would mean opening finished walls. They are slower to use. Parents of young kids often think that is worth it.

    Same-Day Disposal Installation in Norman, OK

    If your old disposal quits with a sink full of water, we can usually install a new one the same day. Our trucks carry common 1/2 and 3/4 HP models, mounting hardware and cord kits, so we do not need a second trip to the supply house before the sink drains again. Most swaps take about an hour.

    Disposal Rough-In for New Construction in Norman

    At rough-in we set the drain height and the outlet location before the cabinets go in. For a kitchen with a disposal, we usually set the drain stub lower than a plain sink would need, so the trap can sit below the disposal outlet with room for proper fall. The electrician adds the switched outlet. We mark its spot so it clears the trap.
