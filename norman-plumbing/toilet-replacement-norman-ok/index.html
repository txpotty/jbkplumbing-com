    Types of Toilet Replacements in Norman, OK

    Cracked Bowl or Tank Replacement in Norman

    Sometimes only the tank is cracked, and if the model is still in production, a matching tank costs less than a whole new toilet. We check the model number first. If the bowl is the cracked part, the whole toilet gets replaced, since the bowl carries the seal to the floor and cannot be patched.

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Norman, OK

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Measure to the closet bolts, not the baseboard. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. An offset flange can correct an inch or two without moving the drain.

    Choosing a Toilet Brand in Norman, OK

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield all make solid toilets, and each line has a few models we steer people away from because of weak flushes or hard-to-find parts. We will tell you which ones. Rough-in and bowl shape decide what fits, and we check both before you buy.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Norman

    Chair height toilets put the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly two inches higher than a standard bowl, and that makes a real difference for knees and hips. The drain stays the same. Many people pair it with an elongated bowl and a soft-close seat, and the swap takes the same couple of hours as any other.

    Toilet Replacement Cost Comparison in Norman, OK

    Three repair calls in a year on the same toilet usually cost more than replacing it, especially when the toilet is old enough to waste water on every flush. We put both numbers side by side. You make the call, and we do not push replacement on a toilet that only needs a flapper.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Norman, OK

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are loud. Replacement means matching the vessel brand for parts later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    Emergency Toilet Replacement in Norman

    A toilet that cracked overnight and is still seeping into the floor cannot wait for next week, so we bring a standard round and elongated model on emergency calls. Shut the angle stop first. Most emergency swaps are done the same day, and we check the subfloor for soaked plywood before setting it.

    Standard Toilet Replacement in Norman

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl. At that point a swap makes more sense than another repair. We pull the old toilet, scrape the flange and check it for cracks, then set the new one on a fresh wax ring with new closet bolts.
