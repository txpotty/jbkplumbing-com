    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Norman, OK

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Usually two small pits. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Either way, the driveway and the trees stay put. We tell you which one your pipe can take.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Norman, OK

    A plugged vent makes drains gurgle and traps run dry. Sewer gas follows soon after.     Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Norman

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. Crushed pipe cannot be lined. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. We see this most in older neighborhoods with clay pipe. We stage the work so you lose sewer service for a few hours, not days.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Norman

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. A cable only punches a hole.
