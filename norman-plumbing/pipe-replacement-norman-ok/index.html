    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Norman, OK

    Many older homes in Norman, OK still have galvanized steel pipes, which are vulnerable to rust and corrosion on the inside. JBK Plumbing provides complete galvanized pipe replacement services, removing these failing pipes and installing new, modern materials. Upgrading from galvanized pipes not only improves your water quality and pressure but also shields your home from potential water damage caused by deteriorating plumbing.

    Whole-Home Repiping in Norman

    If your home in Norman is older and experiencing frequent leaks, low water pressure throughout, or discolored water from multiple fixtures, a whole-home repiping might be the most sensible long-term solution. We can replace all the old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes with modern, durable PEX or copper piping. This upgrades your entire water delivery system, greatly improving water quality and pressure while eliminating future leak concerns from aging infrastructure.

    PEX Repiping in Norman

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for better flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with far fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most requested repipe material.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Norman, OK

    Cast iron drain pipe in older homes can rust through at the bottom, while some ABS from the 1980s was made with defective resin that cracks. Signs include sewer odors, slow drains and stains on ceilings below upstairs bathrooms. We camera the line first, then replace reachable sections with PVC or ABS. Under-slab drains can be tunneled, rerouted or lined depending on location. We explain the options with the camera footage in hand.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Norman

    Slab leaks, common in homes built on concrete foundations, can be damaging and difficult to detect. JBK Plumbing uses advanced leak detection technology to locate the exact source, then expertly repairs or reroutes the compromised pipe, often using durable PEX to bypass the slab entirely, preventing future issues in the same spot.

    Many homes were not built with deep cold in mind, so pipes in attics, exterior walls and garages are particularly vulnerable.     Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Norman, OK
