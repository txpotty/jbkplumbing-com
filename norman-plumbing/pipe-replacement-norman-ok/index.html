    Repiping and Pipe Replacement Options in Norman, OK

    Full Repipes for Older Homes in Norman

    Homes from the 1950s and 1960s often still have original galvanized pipe. That pipe is worn out. A full repipe brings new PEX or copper to every fixture, and fixes pressure and water color at once. Worth it.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Norman

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. Chlorine breaks it down over time. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, which can cause insurance headaches. Replace it.

    Commercial Pipe Replacement in Norman

    Commercial buildings often have larger supply lines and more fixtures. Downtime costs money. We plan pipe replacement in phases whenever the layout allows it, keeping restrooms and kitchens running while we work on other sections. Night work available.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Norman

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. Scale and rust do not affect it. Many rural homes rely on private wells, and some groundwater carries high mineral content that calls for softening or filtration. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, and we run it in home runs from a manifold or in trunk-and-branch layouts. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Water Line Replacement vs. Repair in Norman, OK

    One leak on otherwise good pipe is a repair. Several leaks in different rooms, rusty water throughout, or pipe that crumbles when we cut it tells us the system is failing everywhere, and patching just moves the next leak down the line. We show you what we find.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Norman, OK

    Drain lines fail too. Cast iron cracks and scales. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, checking slope and venting so the new drains flow freely. Camera first.

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Norman, OK

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Small leaks appear in random spots. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, and fixing awkward runs from past remodels. Better flow.

    Pipe Replacement for Multi-Unit Buildings in Norman

    Apartments and condos share risers. One leak can affect several units. We plan riser replacement in phases, one stack at a time, keeping each unit informed. Organized work.
