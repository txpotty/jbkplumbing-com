    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Norman

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi models send a text too. Rentals need these. They can go on a new pump or one that is already working.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Norman, OK

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Norman, OK

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. Swapping a pump in an existing pit is the cheapest job, while a new pit means saw cutting, digging, gravel and a concrete patch, which roughly doubles the labor. Backups add cost. Your written quote shows each option and its price.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Norman

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. We install duplex systems with alternating pumps. Downtime costs money. We plan the install around your business hours.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Norman, OK

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. A smart charger keeps the battery topped off and sounds an alarm if the battery weakens, the primary fails or the water rises past the backup float. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Norman

    No pit yet? We saw-cut the slab at the low corner, break out the concrete and dig down through soil, hardpan or rock to set an 18 by 22 inch basin. We backfill around the basin with washed gravel so groundwater has a path in. Then we patch the slab. The pump, lid and discharge go in last.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Norman

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. We install exterior basins with pumps for those spots. Lots that slope toward the house need this most. We keep the outlet away from your foundation and the neighbor line.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Norman

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. Install time is shorter too. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.
