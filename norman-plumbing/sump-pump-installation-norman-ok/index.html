    A pump replacement in an existing pit usually costs much less than a new pit installation. New pits require slab cutting, excavation and concrete patching, which adds labor.     High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Norman

    A high water alarm alerts you when water rises above normal levels. Smart monitors can send alerts to your phone when the pump runs or fails.     Sump Pump Replacement in Norman, OK

    Sump pumps often sit idle for long stretches between storms, which can lead to stuck floats and seized motors. We replace old pumps with new models and inspect the pit, check valve and discharge line. Upgrading to a higher capacity pump may help if your old one could not keep up. We test the new pump by filling the pit with water. Replacement before storm season is a smart move.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Norman

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can build one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, compacted clay or rock and set a durable basin.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Norman, OK

    Strong storms can knock out power right when your pump is needed most. Battery backup pumps take over automatically when the main pump fails or power goes out.
