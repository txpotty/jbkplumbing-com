    Converting from a tank requires more than swapping units.     Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Norman, OK

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Many rural homes rely on private wells, and some groundwater carries high mineral content that calls for softening or filtration. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides extra protection.
