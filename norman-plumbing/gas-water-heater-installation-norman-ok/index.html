    Traditional Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Norman

    Tank gas heaters are still the workhorse in most garages and utility rooms, usually 40 or 50 gallons. Each install gets a new gas connector and drip leg, a vent connector pitched upward and a relief line piped where code allows. We strap it if code calls for it. Your household sets the size.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Installation in Norman

    Restaurants and laundromats can drain a residential tank in minutes. For businesses we install high-input heaters with bigger gas lines and dedicated vents, sometimes in banks of two or three. Some uses need 140 degrees. We work around your hours.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Norman, OK

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Because the exhaust comes out cool and wet, they vent through PVC and need a condensate drain, which changes where the heater can go and what the install involves. Energy Star models qualify for some rebates. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Norman, OK

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. Without needing a chimney, the vent can exit through a side wall within the allowed length. A 120-volt outlet is required. Termination clearances follow the manual.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Installation in Norman, OK

    Rheem gas heaters show up in a lot of local homes, from basic atmospheric tanks to power vent models. JBK Plumbing handles every Rheem gas model. Our install covers gas, flue and the Guardian safety system, including the air intake that keeps it working. Draft is checked last.

    Proper Sizing and Venting in Norman, OK

    Sizing and venting decide whether a gas heater runs safe and efficient. We size the heater from your peak use, then match the vent to the manufacturer table and code. A small vent can backdraft. We test it every time.

    Propane Water Heater Installation in Norman

    Propane homes need heaters set up for propane, not natural gas. Different pressure and orifice size mean either a propane model or a factory conversion kit. We work with your supplier. We test all fittings.

    Standard Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Norman

    Our standard gas tank install starts with removing the old heater. We drain it, disconnect the gas and the vent, carry it out and then install the new tank with fresh connectors, a new drip leg and a properly pitched vent connector. Testing comes last. They are dependable and affordable.
