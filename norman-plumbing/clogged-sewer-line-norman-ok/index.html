    Sewer Belly and Sagging Pipe Clogs in Norman, OK

    Soil movement can separate sewer lines and water lines over time, so foundation shifts and plumbing problems often go together. A section of sewer pipe that sinks forms a belly. Our camera and locator show where the belly is and how deep it sits. A spot repair restores the slope.
