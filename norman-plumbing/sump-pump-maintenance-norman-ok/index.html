    Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Norman

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacement every three to five years.

    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Norman

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last longer.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Norman, OK

    For homes equipped with pedestal sump pumps, JBK Plumbing provides dedicated maintenance services in Norman, OK. We focus on inspecting the external motor, confirming proper lubrication and checking the impeller for any obstructions. This type of pump, with its motor above the water, requires specific attention to its shaft and bearings to prevent premature wear and ensure reliable performance during heavy rains.

    Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in Norman

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. We test duplex systems, alternating controls and high-water alarms on a scheduled basis. Maintenance logs help property managers stay on top of inspections.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Norman, OK

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil around the slab or foundation.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Norman, OK

    The float switch is a key component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to ensure reliable operation for your home in Norman, OK.
