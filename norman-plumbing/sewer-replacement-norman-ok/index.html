    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Norman

    For significant damage or older, failing systems, traditional sewer line replacement often involves excavating a trench to access and replace the entire pipe. This method is effective for replacing severely collapsed or extensively rooted lines, especially common in older Norman homes with cast iron or clay pipes. We thoughtfully plan the excavation to minimize disruption to your yard and landscaping, using heavy-duty equipment for efficient digging and backfilling.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Norman

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old line while breaking the old pipe apart. It requires only small access pits at each end. This protects landscaping, driveways and patios. It works well for clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe. Most jobs are completed in one day.

    Foundation Protection during Sewer Replacement in Norman, OK

    Soil movement can crack sewer lines and water lines over time, so foundation shifts and plumbing problems often go together. Soil movement can cause foundation shifts, which in turn can damage sewer lines. Our replacement process in Norman, OK takes foundation integrity into consideration. We plan excavations carefully and ensure proper soil compaction and backfill to prevent future issues that could impact both your new sewer line and your home's foundation. We use careful methods to protect your property.

    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Norman, OK

    Many older homes in Norman, OK still have original sewer lines made of clay, cast iron, or Orangeburg pipe, which are prone to degradation, cracking, and root intrusion over time. When we perform a sewer replacement, we often recommend upgrading to modern, durable materials like PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene). These materials are more flexible, resistant to corrosion and roots, and have a much longer lifespan, providing a major upgrade to your home's plumbing infrastructure.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Norman, OK

    When appropriate, trenchless sewer replacement offers a less invasive alternative to traditional digging. Methods like pipe bursting or pipe lining allow us to replace or repair your sewer line with minimal excavation, often only requiring two access points. This can save your lawn, driveway, and landscaping from extensive damage, making it a popular choice for homeowners in Norman who want to preserve their property's appearance. We'll assess if your sewer line is a good candidate for this advanced technique.

    Sewer Scope Inspections for Replacement in Norman

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Norman, OK

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the right option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench after inspection. This method allows full correction of slope problems.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Norman

    Cast iron sewer pipe can corrode from the inside, creating rough surfaces and holes. Scale buildup catches debris and causes clogs. We replace cast iron with PVC or HDPE. This restores flow and ends corrosion. Many homes built before 1975 have cast iron lines.
