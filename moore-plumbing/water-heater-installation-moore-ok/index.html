    Water Heater Installation and Replacement in Moore, OK

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Moore, OK

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match the voltage and breaker size to your panel, and because electric tanks need no vent, they can go in a closet, an attic or a laundry room where a gas unit could not. Closets and attics get a pan. Quiet and simple.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Moore, OK

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. No venting needed. That makes placement easier.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Moore, OK

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. They need roughly 700 cubic feet of air around them and a condensate drain, so a warm garage or a large utility room usually works better than a closet. Rebates may apply. We help you decide if one fits.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Moore

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Every job gets a fresh connector, a drip leg, a shutoff and a vent connector with the right rise toward the chimney or flue. Then a draft test. Big families do well with gas.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Moore, OK

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. We install the gas line, the sediment trap and the vent connector, then check draft at the hood with a match or a smoke pencil to confirm exhaust is going up and out. Then we check for leaks. Expansion tanks go in where code requires.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Moore

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Venting is what keeps CO out. All work follows local code.

    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Moore

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with. Our commercial work covers high-BTU tanks and multi-unit tankless racks, along with the larger gas lines, venting and code inspections that come with them. Downtime costs you money. Night installs are available.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Moore

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. An attic heater needs a pan with its own drain line, a properly routed relief line and enough access to get the old tank out and the new one in. A leak lands on drywall. Relocation to a safer spot is an option.
