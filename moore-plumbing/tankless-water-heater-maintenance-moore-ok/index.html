    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Moore

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading correctly and the burner breathing properly.

    Recirculation and Combi System Service in Moore, OK

    Many tankless units now include built-in recirculation pumps or buffer tanks to deliver faster hot water. These systems move more water through the exchanger and scale up sooner. We flush the recirculation loop, check pump operation and verify crossover or dedicated return line settings. Correct schedules can cut wasted energy while still giving you quick hot water.

    Maintenance Cost Versus Repair Cost in Moore, OK

    A tankless descaling visit usually costs far less than replacing a scaled heat exchanger, which can run $600 to $1,500 or more in parts and labor. Some manufacturers deny exchanger claims when scale damage is found. Regular maintenance helps protect a unit that can last 20 years when cared for. We help you decide whether a softener or scale inhibitor would lower your long-term costs too.

    Isolation Valve Kit Installation in Moore, OK

    Some tankless heaters were installed without service valves, which makes proper flushing impossible. We install a set of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines so future maintenance takes minutes. The kit also lets you shut off water to the heater without closing the main. It is a one-time upgrade that pays for itself in simpler service.

    Commercial Tankless Rack Maintenance in Moore

    Restaurants, salons and multi-family buildings often run several tankless units linked together. Commercial racks see heavy use and scale faster than home units. We descale each heater, check cascade controls and balance the load across the system. Quarterly or semiannual service keeps the hot water flowing during busy hours.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Flushing in Moore

    Much of Oklahoma has moderately hard to very hard water, especially in western areas, which leaves scale in water heaters and fixtures. Hard water can lead to significant mineral buildup in tankless water heaters. Our residential flushing service in Moore involves circulating a descaling solution through your unit's heat exchanger to remove calcium and magnesium deposits.     Navien Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Moore

    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Moore
