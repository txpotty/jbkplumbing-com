    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Moore

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water.     Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Moore

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, usually under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate. JBK Plumbing expertly installs various sizes of HGI units, ensuring proper venting, leveling, and connection to your drainage system in Moore for maximum efficiency.
