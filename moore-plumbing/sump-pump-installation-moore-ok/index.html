    Storm-Ready Sump Pump Work in Moore, OK

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Moore, OK

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. You can reach the motor and the switch without fishing anything out of the pit, which helps in a basement where the pit is shallow or tight. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Moore

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Moore, OK

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Batteries need replacing every few years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Moore, OK

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. We set a basin at the lowest point, run perforated drain pipe to it where needed and seal the vapor barrier around the lid so the space stays dry. Clearance is usually under two feet. The discharge exits through the rim joist and drains away from the house.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Moore

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. We install duplex systems with alternating pumps. A flooded elevator pit shuts down a building. Work is scheduled around your operating hours.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Moore

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. That saves some labor. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Moore

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi monitors go further and send a phone alert when the pump runs too long, the power drops or the water climbs past a set level. Landlords like them. We add them to new installs and to pumps already in place.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Moore

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. Because the motor is cooled by the surrounding water, they run quieter and can push more gallons per minute up a tall lift than most pedestal pumps. The pit lid sits flush. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.
