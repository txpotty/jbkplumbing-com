    Grease Trap Installation in Moore

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Small kitchens can get by with an under-sink trap, but bigger operations often need an in-ground interceptor of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We work from your kitchen plan, set the unit at the right depth and vent it so it passes the pretreatment inspection on the first try.

    Multi-Family and Property Management Plumbing in Moore

    Apartment complexes need a plumber who can handle many units and quick turns between tenants.     Commercial Leak Detection in Moore, OK

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. Zone-by-zone shutoffs and meter readings narrow it down, then acoustic sensors, thermal imaging and tracer gas pinpoint the spot. Running toilets count too. One stuck flush valve can waste several thousand gallons a week.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Rebuilds in Moore

    Most water providers require annual tests on commercial backflow assemblies, and many send late notices that come with fines. Test early. We test reduced pressure zone, double check and pressure vacuum breaker assemblies with gauges calibrated every year and submit the results online or on paper, whichever your provider uses. If a device fails, we usually rebuild it the same day.

    Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in Moore, OK

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while. Once leaks start showing up in different spots every few months, a sectional repipe in Type L copper or commercial-grade PEX usually costs less than the next three emergency calls. We map the system first. Then we phase the work by floor or by tenant so the building stays open.

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Businesses in Moore

    A camera inspection shows the inside of a drain line on a monitor and records it so you can share it with a landlord or contractor. Guesswork goes away. We push the camera from a cleanout, note the footage at every break, root mass or sag, and use a locator to mark depth and position on the ground above. The video is yours to keep. That record makes capital planning far easier when the board asks why the sewer needs money.

    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in Moore, OK

    Restroom calls are the most common commercial work we do. Running flush valves, stuck urinals and dripping sensor faucets usually need a diaphragm, a solenoid or fresh batteries. We stock those. Clogged wall-hung toilets sometimes need the carrier checked, because a loose bowl on a carrier can leak behind the wall. Weak flow at one sink often comes down to a plugged stop valve.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Moore

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. We use high-pressure jetting with rotating nozzles that scrub grease off the whole circumference of 4-inch and 6-inch lines, not just the bottom. A jetted line stays open far longer than a cabled one. We see the most benefit in restaurants, schools and multi-family stacks.
