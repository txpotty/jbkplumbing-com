    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Moore

    Older homes were plumbed with galvanized steel, which rusts from the inside out. Pressure fades over years. Every time a fitting is disturbed, the next one tends to leak, so repairs on galvanized are usually short-term and replacing the run with PEX or copper is the lasting fix. We use dielectric unions at transitions.

    Underground Pipe Repair in Moore

    Buried pipe takes stress from settling soil, roots and vehicles. Leaks hide well. We locate underground leaks with acoustic equipment and tracer gas, and either spot repair at the leak or replace the line by pulling new pipe through. Every repair gets a pressure test.

    Pipe Repair vs Repipe Cost in Moore, OK

    A single pipe repair costs far less than a whole-house repipe. Spot fixes suit healthy systems. If you are seeing repeated leaks, low pressure or rusty water, the total cost of patching can pass the cost of repiping within a few years, which is why we price both and give you our honest take. The decision is yours.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Moore

    Galvanized steel and old copper both corrode with age. Brown water is a clue. Low pressure, discolored water and leaks at threaded joints tell us the pipe is closing off from the inside, and we replace the worst sections or the whole run with PEX or copper and dielectric fittings at every transition. Pressure comes right back.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair in Moore, OK
