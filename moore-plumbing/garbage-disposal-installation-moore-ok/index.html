    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Moore, OK

    On a double sink, the disposal goes on one bowl and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. The layout keeps both bowls draining and stops the disposal from pushing water into the second bowl, a problem when the tee is too close to the discharge. Heavy-wall fittings last longer.

    Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Moore

    New disposals come with a knockout plug in the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp. Forgetting the plug floods the dishwasher. An air gap or high loop goes in too, which keeps sink water from running back into the dishwasher.

    Replacing an Old Disposal in Moore, OK
