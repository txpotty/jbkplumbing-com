    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines.     Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Moore

These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition.
