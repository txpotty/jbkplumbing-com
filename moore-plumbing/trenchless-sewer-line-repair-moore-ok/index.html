    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Moore, OK

    Sewer lines in Moore, OK can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or misalign. Expansive red clay is common in Oklahoma, swelling when wet and shrinking in drought, which shifts foundations and buried pipe. Tree root intrusion is another major culprit, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also contribute to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Moore, OK

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is well suited for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a fast and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Oklahoma homeowners.

    Sewer Camera Inspections for Trenchless Repair in Moore

    Before any trenchless repair, a detailed sewer camera inspection is essential. Our high-definition cameras travel through your sewer lines, providing real-time video of the pipe's interior. This allows us to precisely diagnose the problem, identify its exact location and determine the most appropriate trenchless method, whether it is pipe bursting or CIPP. This diagnostic step makes for a precise and effective repair in Moore.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Moore

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while fracturing the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is highly resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Moore homes. This method is ideal when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Moore, OK

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is particularly useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add many years of service.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Moore

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We determine the material on camera before recommending a method.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Moore

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot penetrate. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their form.
