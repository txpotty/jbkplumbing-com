    Slab Leak Detection and Repair Cost Comparison in Moore, OK

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Moore

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is popular when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Slab Leak Detection in Moore

    The cost for professional slab leak detection in Moore, OK usually ranges from $250 to $700, depending on the complexity of the leak and the time required for diagnosis. This initial detection fee is separate from any repair costs, which can vary widely based on the leak's location and required pipe repair.

    Common signs of a slab leak include surprisingly high water bills, warm spots on your floor, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, visible cracks in the foundation, damp or wet spots on carpets or flooring and a sudden drop in water pressure. Musty odors can also indicate moisture buildup.

    Common pipe materials prone to slab leaks include older copper pipes vulnerable to corrosion from hard water or soil acidity, and sometimes PEX or CPVC if they were improperly installed or exposed to extreme temperatures. Galvanized steel pipes, though less common under slabs today, can also corrode and leak.

    Most slab leak detection services can be completed within 1 to 3 hours, depending on the severity and location of the leak. Our goal is always to be as efficient and non-invasive as possible, providing a quick diagnosis so repair work can begin soon.

    Once the slab leak is precisely detected, our technician will provide you with a detailed report and discuss the best repair options. These options may include trenchless repair, re-routing the affected pipe or traditional slab excavation and repair. We will then provide a transparent estimate for the repair work.

    Slab leaks can cause serious damage including foundation cracks, soil erosion beneath the slab, mold and mildew growth in wall cavities or under flooring, damage to flooring materials and increased humidity levels. Over time, it can lead to structural instability and major, costly repairs.

    We stand by the accuracy of our slab leak detection services. While detection itself is a diagnostic service, we make sure our findings are precise. Any subsequent repairs performed by JBK Plumbing come with our standard labor and parts warranties, which will be clearly outlined for you.

    While the detection process itself typically doesn't require a permit, any subsequent repair work involving the excavation of the slab or significant plumbing alterations will likely require a plumbing permit from the local building department.
