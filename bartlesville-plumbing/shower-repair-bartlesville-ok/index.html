    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Bartlesville, OK

    Weak shower pressure is often caused by mineral buildup in the showerhead or flow restrictor. It can also come from a clogged cartridge or a failing pressure regulator. We clean or replace the showerhead, check the cartridge and test whole-home pressure if needed. Most low-pressure issues are fixed quickly.

    Clogged Shower Drain Solutions in Bartlesville, OK

    Slow or standing water in your shower is a clear sign of a clogged drain. These clogs are usually caused by a buildup of hair, soap scum, and sometimes mineral deposits. Our team uses professional drain snakes and hydro-jetting equipment to safely and effectively clear even the most stubborn blockages without damaging your pipes. We also offer advice on preventive measures to keep your shower drains flowing freely.

    Leaky Shower Head Repair in Bartlesville

    A dripping shower head wastes water and can be very annoying. Our plumbers can quickly identify the cause, whether it is a worn-out washer, O-ring, or a faulty cartridge.     Showerhead and Arm Repair in Bartlesville

    Dripping showerheads, leaking arm connections and broken handheld hoses are common problems. We clean or replace showerheads, reseal shower arms and install new handheld hoses and holders.
