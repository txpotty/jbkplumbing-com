    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Bartlesville, OK

    Switching from a tank is more than a swap. A typical 40-gallon gas tank uses around 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the gas line from the meter almost always gets upsized. Venting changes too. We plan both on the quote.

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Bartlesville, OK

    Upfront, a tankless install in Bartlesville, OK costs more than a standard tank because of the unit price, the gas line upgrade and the venting changes. Over the long haul, the math narrows. Lower standby loss, a lifespan of up to 20 years against about 10 for a tank, and parts that can be replaced instead of the whole unit all count toward the total cost of ownership. Ask for both prices.
