    Root Intrusion Removal in Bartlesville, OK

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. They come back fast.     Sewer Pipe Relining in Bartlesville, OK

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. For most houses the flow difference is negligible.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Bartlesville

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. We record the whole run.     Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Bartlesville

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. Dirt falls in through the crack.     Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Bartlesville

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.
