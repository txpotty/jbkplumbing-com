    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Bartlesville

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part in your water heater designed to corrode instead of the tank itself. Depending on your water chemistry, these rods can wear down faster than expected. During maintenance, we inspect the anode rod's condition and recommend replacement if necessary. A healthy anode rod is key for preventing rust and extending the lifespan of your water heater tank, saving you from a costly full replacement.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Bartlesville

    For electric water heaters, our maintenance service focuses on checking heating elements, thermostats, and electrical connections. We confirm proper wiring and test for any short circuits or power problems that could affect performance. Sediment flushing is also performed, just like with gas models, to prevent buildup and protect the lower heating element from burning out early in Bartlesville.

    Attic and Garage Water Heater Maintenance in Bartlesville

    Many homes have water heaters in garages or attics, where heat, cold and tight access add stress to the unit. Attic units need working drain pans and drain lines to prevent ceiling damage if the tank leaks. We check the pan, drain line, venting and access platform. Garage units should be raised and protected where required for gas appliances. We confirm your setup meets local code.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Bartlesville, OK

    Tankless water heaters need specific maintenance, mainly a descaling flush to remove mineral deposits that can restrict flow and reduce efficiency. Our technicians in Bartlesville, OK use commercial-grade solutions to clean the heat exchanger, so your unit keeps providing on-demand hot water efficiently. We also inspect the igniter, fan, and exhaust venting for optimal performance and safety.
