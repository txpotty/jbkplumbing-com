    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Bartlesville, OK

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. High pressure ages fixtures fast. Anything over 80 psi cuts the life of hoses, fixtures and heaters, and we note the supply pipe type, from copper and CPVC to poly, Kitec and galvanized.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspections in Bartlesville, OK

    A sewer camera shows the inside of the main drain without digging. The camera goes in at a cleanout and travels to the main or the tank, recording roots, cracks, offsets and bellies along the way. A locator marks the depth. Soil movement can separate sewer lines and water lines over time, so foundation shifts and plumbing problems often go together.
