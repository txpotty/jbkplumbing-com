    Expansion Tank Service in Bartlesville, OK

    A thermal expansion tank is the small steel tank on the cold line above the heater, and it gives heated water somewhere to go when a check valve or pressure reducer closes the system.     Anode Rod Replacement in Bartlesville, OK

    Water chemistry changes from one street to the next, and so does anode life. Three to five years is typical. A softened water supply can chew through a magnesium rod in two.     Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Bartlesville, OK

    The anode rod is a sacrificial metal rod that corrodes so the steel tank does not. Many rural homes rely on private wells, and some groundwater carries high mineral content that benefits from softening or filtration. We break the hex head loose, pull the rod and check how much bare wire shows. Six inches of bare wire means replace it.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Bartlesville

    Calcium carbonate drops out of hard water every time the tank heats, and over a year it can harden into chunks the size of a thumbnail. That is what makes the popping noise. When a plain flush will not move them, we use a descaling solution rated for potable systems, let it sit and then flush it all out. A clean bottom puts less heat stress on the seam.
