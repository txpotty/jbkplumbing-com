    Float Switch Inspection in Bartlesville, OK

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. We watch the float through a full cycle. Next we adjust the on and off levels so each run is longer and the starts are fewer.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Bartlesville

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. The backup takes over when the primary loses power or simply stops. We load test the battery, check the charger and the alarm, and run the backup by itself with the main pump unplugged. Expect a new battery every three to five years.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Bartlesville, OK

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. That is the moment a backup pump earns its keep. We check the battery date, clean the terminals and confirm the charger is actually charging, since a dead charger quietly drains the battery to nothing over a few months.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Bartlesville

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. A failed valve sends that water back down for the pump to move again. Motors wear out faster. Our plumber tests the valve, confirms there is a weep hole below it to prevent air lock, and checks each joint to the wall.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Bartlesville, OK

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, so the wear shows up in the shaft, the bearings and the float rod. The float rod gets checked for bends, and the stops get checked for slipping. Bearings get a listen. A motor that whines or rattles on startup is usually close to done.

    Emergency Sump Pump Service in Bartlesville
