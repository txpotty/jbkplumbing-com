    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Bartlesville, OK

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. On cast iron, the bolt slots rust out first.     Rough-In Toilet Installation in Bartlesville, OK

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. On a toilet, that is a vented 3 inch drain and a cold water stub. We set the drain 12 inches from the framed wall plus the thickness of drywall, so the finished rough-in lands right where the fixture expects it. Then the inspector sees it open.

    Smart Toilet and Bidet Seat Installation in Bartlesville

    Bidet seats like the TOTO Washlet and Bio Bidet bolt onto most existing elongated bowls. A T-valve splits water off the fill line. Non-electric seats take about thirty minutes.

    Standard Toilet Installation in Bartlesville
