    Pipe Bursting Services in Midwest City, OK

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. The new line takes its place. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, while a continuous HDPE pipe is pulled in behind at the same diameter or bigger. Collapsed sections are fine. Lining cannot do that.

    Every repair is warrantied. The terms are in writing. Our workmanship warranty covers the repair, while the parts carry separate manufacturer coverage. We explain it before we start.

    In most cities, the homeowner owns that line. The utility owns the meter. The utility usually maintains the meter and the pipe on the street side, and we can help confirm which side of the meter the leak is on before anyone digs. It matters for the bill.

    Check the meter with every fixture off. Look at the leak indicator. If it moves, water is going somewhere, and a wet or sinking spot in the yard or a hissing sound near the meter box usually points to the buried service. A locate visit settles it.

    Acoustic ground microphones pick up the sound of escaping water. Tracer gas helps too. Hydrogen-nitrogen gas pushed into the line rises through the soil at the leak, and electronic locators trace the exact pipe route so we know where to listen. The mark is usually very close.

    For water lines we use PEX or Type K copper. Both are rated for burial. Sewer and drain work uses Schedule 40 PVC, and trenchless jobs use fused HDPE, all of which resist corrosion and handle ground movement better than the old materials they replace. We follow your city's code.
