    Grease Trap and Interceptor Repair in Midwest City, OK

    Food service sites need a grease trap or interceptor to keep fats, oils and grease out of the public sewer, and pretreatment inspectors check them. Odor is the first warning. Our work includes baffle and outlet tee repair, lid replacement, inlet line jetting and a sizing check against your fixtures and flow. Slow sinks and overflow usually point to a full or damaged trap. We remind you to keep the pump-out log current for the inspector.

    Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in Midwest City, OK

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while. Once leaks start showing up in different spots every few months, a sectional repipe in Type L copper or commercial-grade PEX usually costs less than the next three emergency calls. The first step is mapping the risers and branches. Then we phase the work by floor or by tenant so the building stays open.

    Commercial Sewer Line Repair in Midwest City, OK

    The building sewer on a commercial property carries everything from every tenant, so a root intrusion or a belly in the line affects the whole property. We camera the line, locate the problem depth with a sonde and then decide between a spot repair, pipe lining or pipe bursting. Trenchless saves parking lots. Lining a 6-inch lateral can often be done in a day without tearing up asphalt or closing an entrance.

    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in Midwest City, OK

    Restroom calls are the most common commercial work we do. A flushometer that runs, a urinal that will not stop, or a sensor faucet that drips are usually fixed with a diaphragm kit, a solenoid or a new battery pack. We stock those. Wall-hung toilets that keep clogging or rocking get a carrier check, since a loose bowl can leak inside the wall. Weak flow at one sink often comes down to a plugged stop valve.

    Commercial Fixture and Faucet Repair in Midwest City, OK

    Commercial faucets and toilets are built heavy, but they still wear. Cartridges leak. Sensor and metering faucets, wall-hung toilets, mop sinks and compartment sinks are routine for us, with Sloan, Chicago Faucets, Zurn and T and S Brass parts on hand. Low-flow aerators cut water use without slowing hand washing. Most fixture calls are done in under two hours.

    Commercial Water Heaters and Boilers in Midwest City, OK

    Gyms, commercial kitchens and apartment buildings burn through hot water in short, heavy peaks. Our work covers high-input tanks, multi-unit tankless racks and boilers feeding indirect storage tanks. Much of Oklahoma has moderately hard to very hard water, especially in western areas, which leaves scale in water heaters and fixtures. Mineral scale chokes output quickly. We set up a flushing and descaling schedule, and we install thermostatic mixing valves so hand sinks deliver tempered water without scalding anyone.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Midwest City

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. We read each check valve and the relief valve on a calibrated gauge and record the numbers on the standard test form. We tag every passing device. We file the report with your water provider and keep a copy on our side for next year.

    Grease Interceptor and Line Service in Midwest City, OK

Our crew checks the baffles and tees, replaces what is broken and jets the feed lines to strip hardened grease a cable cannot touch. Regular jetting means fewer backups during the dinner rush.
