    Standard tank heaters remain the most common type, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment.     Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Midwest City

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. Since tankless can pull four times the gas of a tank, the meter line usually gets bigger, and the old vent is swapped for stainless or PVC. We price both options.

    Water Heater Replacement Cost Comparison in Midwest City, OK

    A standard tank replacement is usually the most affordable option in Midwest City, OK.     Electric Water Heater Replacement in Midwest City

    Electric water heaters in Midwest City usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Hot water is back the same day.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Midwest City, OK

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Midwest City, OK homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Then we leak test.
