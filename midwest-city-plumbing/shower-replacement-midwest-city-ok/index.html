    Commercial Shower Replacement in Midwest City

    Gyms and hotels need showers that survive heavy use. Commercial replacements get heavy-duty valves, tamper-resistant trim and properly sized drains to code. Work is phased. That way some showers stay open while others are replaced.

    Custom Tile Shower Replacement in Midwest City, OK

    Custom tile replacement starts with careful demolition. Demo goes to the studs, damaged wood gets replaced and a new waterproofed pan goes in with the valve set to tile depth. The slope has to be right. We flood-test before tile.

    Tile Shower Replacement in Midwest City, OK

    Grout cracks and failed waterproofing are how tile showers die. Our job is the valve, drain and pan, and we coordinate with the tile installer on the waterproofing membrane. Schluter Kerdi is a common choice. It goes on over the backer board.

    Accessible Shower Replacement in Midwest City, OK

    Barrier-free showers need low or flat entries and controls within reach. The valve goes within reach of a seat, blocking goes in for grab bars and a handheld goes on a slide bar. Entries stay wide. Seats can be built in or fold-down.
