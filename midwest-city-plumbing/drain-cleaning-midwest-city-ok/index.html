    Hydro Jetting for Drains in Midwest City

    Jetting does what a cable cannot. Water at around 3,000 to 4,000 PSI scours the whole pipe wall. A cable punches a hole, while a jetter cleans the pipe back to near its original diameter. It works best on a kitchen or main line that keeps clogging every few months no matter how often it gets cabled.

    Clogged Toilet Repair in Midwest City, OK

    Toilets clog at the trapway, a tight bend inside the porcelain. We use a closet auger with a rubber guard on the bowl end. If the toilet clears but water still rises in the tub, the clog is past the toilet in the branch or main, and we move to a bigger machine at that point. Kids' toys come out too.

    Main Line Drain Cleaning in Midwest City

    Toilets bubbling while the washer drains? The main line is the likely spot. Every fixture shares that one pipe, so the lowest drain in the house is where water comes up, and the usual causes are roots, a crushed section of old pipe or a heap of wipes caught on a bad joint, which we find with a camera and clear with a sectional cable or a jetter.

    Jetting Versus Cabling in Midwest City, OK

    Cabling and jetting solve different problems. A sectional cable is built to chop and pull a clog out. A jetter scrubs the pipe wall clean and flushes the debris downstream to the city main or septic tank, so we cable first on a hard root mass and jet afterward when the line has years of buildup on it.
