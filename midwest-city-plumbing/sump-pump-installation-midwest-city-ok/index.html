    Sump Pump Replacement in Midwest City, OK

    Sump pumps often sit idle for long stretches between storms, which can lead to stuck floats and seized motors. We test the new pump by filling the pit with water. Replacement before storm season is a smart move.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Midwest City, OK

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and repair. Our team is skilled in installing these pumps, making sure of proper pit depth and discharge line setup for reliable performance in Midwest City, OK.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Midwest City, OK

    Power outages during heavy storms are a serious concern, which is why a battery backup sump pump is a smart addition.     New Sump Pit Installation in Midwest City

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can build one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, compacted clay or rock and set a durable basin.     Some homes collect water in sunken patios, below-grade driveways or window wells rather than a basement.
