    How We Replace Polybutylene in Midwest City, OK

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Midwest City

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. Copper is stiff, heat tolerant and long proven. It costs more. Some water can pinhole copper over time, so we look at your water and any past leak history before recommending it over PEX.

    Emergency Polybutylene Pipe Repair in Midwest City, OK

    A burst poly fitting can push water across a floor or through a ceiling in minutes. Shut the main first. We stop the water, splice in a temporary repair to restore service and then plan the full repipe on a calm schedule. We take photos for the adjuster.

    Full Home Polybutylene Repiping in Midwest City

    A full replacement takes every poly supply line out of service, not just the sections that have failed. Partial jobs leave risk behind. New pipe goes from the main to every fixture and appliance, each one gets a new quarter-turn stop and the system is tested before any wall is closed.

    Polybutylene Checks for Home Sales in Midwest City, OK

    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Midwest City

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. Start at the water heater. It also shows up under sinks, at the main and through garage walls, and we follow it into the attic to see how much of the house it feeds.

    Insurance and Home Sale Documentation in Midwest City

    Some insurers decline or surcharge policies on homes with polybutylene. Buyers ask about it too. Once the work is done you get the permit, invoice and photos, which is what agents and buyers ask to see.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Midwest City

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. For slab houses, new PEX-A runs through the attic and drops down interior walls, so none of it sits under the floor. PEX handles a freeze better than copper. Expansion fittings keep the bore full size, which helps pressure at the last fixture on the line.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Midwest City, OK

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Labor runs lower with PEX. We keep PEX out of direct sunlight, away from water heater flues and recessed light housings and support it at the spacing the manufacturer calls for, which protects both the pipe and the warranty.
