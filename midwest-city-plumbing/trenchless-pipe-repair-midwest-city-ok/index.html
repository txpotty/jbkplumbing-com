    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Midwest City, OK

    Pipe bursting is another trenchless method used for replacing badly damaged pipes. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is well suited for completely replacing a pipe segment with a new, stronger material, such as HDPE. It's often chosen when the existing pipe is collapsed or too damaged for lining. JBK Plumbing uses specialized bursting heads and winches for a smooth and efficient replacement, reducing the overall project time and cost for homeowners in Midwest City, OK.

    CIPP (cured-in-place pipe) lining involves inserting a new liner inside the existing pipe to create a pipe within a pipe. Pipe bursting, on the other hand, involves breaking apart the old pipe while pulling a new pipe into its place, resulting in a completely new pipe.
