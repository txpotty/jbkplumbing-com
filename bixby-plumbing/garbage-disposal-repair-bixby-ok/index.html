    The cost of garbage disposal repair in Bixby, OK can range from $100 to $300, depending on the issue.     While some minor issues like resetting the unit or clearing a simple jam with an Allen wrench can be DIY, more complex problems involving electrical components, significant leaks, or motor issues are best left to a professional.     The reset button protects the motor from overheating. If it trips often, the unit may be overloaded, jammed or failing.

    Frequent jams are usually caused by putting fibrous foods like celery, potato peels, or corn husks down the drain, or by disposing of non-food items. Hard items like bones or fruit pits can also lead to jams. Using plenty of cold water while grinding helps prevent buildup.
