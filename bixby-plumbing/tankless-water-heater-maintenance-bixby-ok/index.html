    Tankless Banks for Restaurants and Apartments in Bixby

    Cascade systems link several tankless units to one controller that fires them in turn. They see heavy use, and scale builds faster than at home. Each heater gets descaled, and we check that the controller spreads the load evenly. Quarterly service is common.

    Commercial Tankless Maintenance in Bixby, OK

    Commercial kitchens and salons burn through hot water all day, so their tankless units scale up much faster than a house unit. Gas pressure gets checked with every burner lit, not at idle. We pull and clean the inlet screens and air filters. Downtime is the real cost. We can book it before you open or after you close.

    Recirculation and Combi Unit Service in Bixby, OK

    Built-in recirculation pumps and buffer tanks get hot water to the tap faster, but they also push more water through the exchanger every day, so those units scale sooner. The recirc loop gets flushed with the exchanger. Next we confirm crossover valve or dedicated return, and set the pump schedule to fit.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Bixby

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. The secondary exchanger produces acidic water. The condensate trap gets cleared and the neutralizer media checked. A-series models with built-in recirculation also get a pump and buffer tank check.

    Tankless Vent Inspection in Bixby

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house. On a condensing unit the PVC or polypropylene vent should pitch back toward the heater. Every joint gets checked, and we test for carbon monoxide by the heater. Wasp nests in vent caps are common.

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Bixby, OK

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. We read gas pressure at the inlet with a manometer while the burner is at full fire and compare it to the rating plate. We check the flue for blockages. You get a written report with the readings, so next year's visit has something to compare against.

    Home Tankless Flushing in Bixby

    Much of Oklahoma has moderately hard to very hard water, especially in western areas, which leaves scale in water heaters and fixtures. Inside a tankless heater, that water runs through a small copper or stainless coil while the burner fires, and every minute of hot water leaves a little calcium behind. We hook a pump to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for about an hour. The flow returns. Temperatures stop swinging, and the unit quits shutting down on high-limit.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Bixby

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is the exchanger. Minerals settle on the hottest tube walls first, and the passages get narrower. We dissolve it with 45 to 60 minutes of pump-driven descaling solution.
