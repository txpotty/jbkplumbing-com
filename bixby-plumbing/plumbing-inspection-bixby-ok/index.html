    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Bixby, OK

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Bixby

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked. We also look at the expansion tank, drain pan and softener, and a hardness test shows whether the softener is working or just eating salt.

    Leak Detection and Assessment in Bixby, OK

    Hidden leaks waste water and quietly damage floors and walls. Start at the meter. With all fixtures off, we watch the meter's leak indicator, then use acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera to trace the leak behind walls, under floors or in the slab. Early detection limits the damage. Expansive red clay is common in Oklahoma, swelling when wet and shrinking in drought, which shifts foundations and buried pipe.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Bixby

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. We test the T and P valve, confirm its discharge line runs to a safe place, look for an expansion tank where one is needed and check the drain pan. Gas units get a CO check. Backdrafting heaters can push combustion gases indoors, which is why we test the vent and report problems before we leave.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Bixby, OK

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. Old brass connectors, missing shutoffs or drip legs and rust all get noted, and we sweep joints with a gas detector. Any leak is reported on the spot. Most of our gas findings are in older homes, often at original appliance connectors.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Bixby, OK

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Some need replacing soon. Poly and Kitec fail suddenly and galvanized chokes off with rust, so we flag them with replacement options.

    Plumbing Inspection Costs in Bixby, OK

    A basic plumbing inspection usually costs $150 to $300, and adding a sewer camera usually brings it to $250 to $500. Larger homes are priced by scope. Next to a surprise sewer replacement at $4,000 to $15,000 or more, an inspection costs little, and the price is set when you schedule.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Bixby, OK

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. We test each valve. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Bixby, OK

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. We run a high-resolution camera from a cleanout to the city connection or septic tank. The video shows offsets, bellies, roots and breaks along with their depth and location, and you keep a copy of the recording for your records or for a contractor.
