    Standard Toilet Replacement in Bixby

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl. At that point a swap makes more sense than another repair. The old unit comes off, the flange gets scraped clean and checked for cracks, then set the new one on a fresh wax ring with new closet bolts.

    Emergency Toilet Replacement in Bixby

    A toilet that cracked overnight and is still seeping into the floor cannot wait for next week, so we bring a standard round and elongated model on emergency calls. Shut the angle stop first. Most of the time the new toilet goes in that same day, and we check the subfloor for soaked plywood before setting it.

    Toilet Replacement Cost Comparison in Bixby, OK

    Three repair calls in a year on the same toilet usually cost more than replacing it, especially when the toilet is old enough to waste water on every flush. We put both numbers side by side. You make the call, and we do not push replacement on a toilet that only needs a flapper.

    What a Standard Swap Includes in Bixby

    The swap itself runs about two hours: water off, tank drained, old toilet out, flange scraped and inspected, new toilet set on a fresh seal, and a braided stainless supply line connected. Then the leveling. The base gets caulked, with a small gap left at the back so any future leak shows up instead of hiding under the floor.

    Commercial Flush Valve Upgrades in Bixby

    Manual flush valves on older commercial toilets leak at the handle and stick open, and swapping to a sensor valve during a replacement improves hygiene without changing the rough-in. Battery and hardwired models are both available. Work can happen after hours, and we check that the new fixtures meet accessibility requirements.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Bixby, OK

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are noticeably louder than gravity toilets. Replacement means matching the vessel brand for parts later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Bixby, OK

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They cost more and weigh more. A one-piece toilet usually takes two people to carry upstairs, and we set it without dragging it across your tile so the floor stays unscratched and the wax ring lands centered on the flange.

    Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Bixby, OK

    Older toilets are one of the largest indoor water users in a house, and replacing a 3.5 gallon model with a 1.28 gallon one cuts toilet water use by more than half. Dual-flush toilets add a lighter flush for liquids. We recommend models with high MaP scores so the savings do not come with double flushing.

    Watch for cracks, weekly clogs, a base that rocks after it has been reset, and a tank that uses 3.5 gallons or more. The age of the toilet matters too. Toilets past 25 years old are often worth replacing even if nothing is broken yet.
