    Sewer Line Repair Work We Handle in Bixby, OK

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Bixby, OK

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. On a four-inch residential line, that rarely matters.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in Bixby

    Multifamily buildings and offices put several units' worth of flow through one lateral. Minor sags clog quickly under that load. We repair larger diameter lines, add two-way cleanouts so future maintenance is easy, and give managers recorded footage for owners, insurers and their own files. Night and weekend work is available.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Bixby

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Bixby, OK

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. The whole house loses its drains. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and the camera tells us exactly where the pipe gave way and how deep it sits. We dig at the mark and replace the broken length with PVC. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Bixby

    Every repair starts with a look inside. We run the camera from the cleanout. You get a copy of the video and a plain written summary of each defect with its distance from the house, which helps if you are buying a home, filing a claim or getting a second opinion on a quote someone else gave you. It often saves people from repairs they do not need.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Bixby, OK

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. The joint is the real problem. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, based on the number of bad joints and the spacing between them. One or two joints is a small job. A line rooted end to end is a lining or replacement job.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Bixby

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease and wipes cause most of it. We jet the line, camera it, and repair the weak section with options that limit how long you are closed, such as overnight work or temporary bypass pumps that keep the building running. We also install two-way cleanouts for future maintenance.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Bixby

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. The crack lets soil in and sewage out. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded stainless couplings rated for the transition between materials, then test the repair with water and video before closing the hole. Figure on a single day for a typical spot repair.
