    Sewer Camera Inspection in Bixby

    Every sewer repair starts with a look inside the pipe. We run a self-leveling camera from your cleanout or a pulled toilet and record the full length of the line. A sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact location and depth of every problem on the surface. You get a copy of the video and a plain-language summary of what we found. This step alone frequently saves homeowners from paying for repairs they do not need.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Bixby, OK

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies usually form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. Most homes sit on concrete slabs, so leaks under the floor often call for slab leak detection before repair. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is usually a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Bixby

    When trenchless repair isn't feasible due to severe damage or specific pipe configurations, traditional dig-and-replace methods are necessary. This involves excavating a trench to access and replace the damaged section of the sewer line. While more intrusive, it is sometimes the only way to address severely collapsed pipes, significant offsets or certain pipe materials common in older Oklahoma properties. Our team makes sure of careful excavation and efficient replacement, minimizing the impact on your property in Bixby.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Bixby, OK

    While often overlooked, the sewer vent pipe is crucial for proper drainage and preventing sewer gas buildup. Issues with vent pipes, such as blockages from animal nests or damage from strong winds, can lead to slow drains, gurgling noises and foul odors. JBK Plumbing diagnoses and repairs vent pipe problems, making sure your entire drainage system works correctly and safely and maintaining good indoor air quality.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Bixby

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's far more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and preventing recurring clogs. This service is especially helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Bixby dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with recurring drain issues.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Bixby

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to recurring clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it holds.

    Sewer Line Backups & Blockages in Bixby

    Sewer backups are usually caused by severe blockages from grease buildup, foreign objects or extensive tree root intrusion. Our team uses HD sewer camera inspections to identify the exact cause and location of the blockage. We then use hydro-jetting or mechanical snaking to clear the obstruction, restoring proper flow to your system and preventing further damage. This approach is highly effective for the stubborn clogs often found in older Oklahoma homes.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Bixby, OK

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an excellent option for preserving landscapes in Bixby.
