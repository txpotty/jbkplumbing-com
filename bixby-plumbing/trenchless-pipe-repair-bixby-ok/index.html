    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Bixby

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Both matter. Picote milling heads clear roots, scale and old fittings out of the pipe before lining, and after the liner cures, they cut open the lateral connections so every drain in your home flows again. A clean pipe holds a liner best.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Bixby

    Tree roots, corrosion and soil movement wreck most sewer lines. Soil movement can separate sewer lines and water lines over time, so foundation shifts and plumbing problems often go together. We fix minor cracks with a spot liner. For longer damage we line the whole run, and for a collapse we burst in new pipe, without trenching the yard or cutting the drive.

    Commercial Trenchless Pipe Repair in Bixby

    Commercial properties have sewer and storm lines under parking lots, sidewalks and buildings. Tearing it out costs plenty.     Pipe Bursting in Bixby, OK

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. The head fractures the old pipe as it travels, with new HDPE trailing behind, fused into one length with no joints.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Bixby, OK

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It also upsizes lines. Our crew sets an entry pit and an exit pit, then pulls a bursting head and new HDPE through on a hydraulic winch, which replaces the full run in a day or two for most homes. JBK Plumbing joins the new pipe by heat fusion.

    Lateral and Spot Lining in Bixby

    Not every pipe needs lining end to end. Short damage gets a short liner. A sectional liner is cheaper and quicker for a short stretch of damage, though we camera the rest of the line first to be sure it is sound.
