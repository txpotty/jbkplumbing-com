    Toilet and Fixture Leak Detection in Bixby, OK

We use dye tests to find leaking toilets and check faucets, showers and supply lines for drips. These fixes are typically quick and save water right away.

    Smart Leak Detection Device Installation in Bixby

    Smart shutoff valves like Moen Flo and Phyn monitor water flow and can shut off water automatically when they detect a leak.     Pinhole Leak Detection in Bixby, OK

    Pinhole leaks, often found in copper or PEX piping, are small but can cause serious damage over time, especially behind walls or under floors. We use non-invasive moisture meters to detect elevated humidity levels and thermal cameras to identify cold spots that indicate water. Our plumbers are skilled at tracing these elusive leaks without tearing apart your entire home. Swift action on pinhole leaks prevents extensive drywall damage, mold growth and compromised structural integrity. Hot summers and sudden temperature swings are the main climate stress in Oklahoma, putting extra strain on pipes, joints and water heaters.

    Water Meter Leak Testing in Bixby

    The water meter is the first and easiest leak test. With every fixture and appliance off, a moving leak indicator means water is escaping somewhere. We read the flow rate to estimate how many gallons are being lost. Then we shut off zones one at a time to see which part of the property is losing water.
