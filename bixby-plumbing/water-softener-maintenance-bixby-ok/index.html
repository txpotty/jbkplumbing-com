    Control Valve and Injector Service in Bixby, OK

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw.     Regeneration Settings and Hardness Testing in Bixby, OK

    Many softeners are set incorrectly from day one or never updated when household size changes. We test your raw water hardness and program the valve to match your actual use.
