    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Bixby, OK

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Bixby, OK, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers expertly install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also make sure of proper clearance and drainage.
