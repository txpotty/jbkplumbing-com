    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Bixby

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Little upkeep is needed. They work best on moderate water.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Bixby

    Testing is what makes the settings right. We test for hardness, iron and chlorine before installing, then program the regeneration frequency and salt dose so you get soft water without wasting salt. Iron adds to the setting. We review the settings with you before we leave.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Bixby, OK

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. JBK Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. They work best on very hard water.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Bixby

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. That pays back over time. Several models are available.

    Softener Loop Installation in Bixby

    No loop? A loop is the plumbing that routes the main line through a softener, and many older homes were built without one. We add the loop. Where we can, we keep hose bibs and irrigation on hard water to save salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Bixby, OK

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops.
