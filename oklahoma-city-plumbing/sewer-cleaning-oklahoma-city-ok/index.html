    Residential Sewer Cleaning in Oklahoma City

    Household sewers clog slowly. Grease, hair and wipes build up over months. Debris catches at a rough joint or a sag, and every load of laundry adds to it until the line closes, and we clear it with a sectional cable or a small jetter sized for the pipe.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Oklahoma City, OK

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Much of the housing in Oklahoma dates from the mid-century boom onward, with many older homes on slabs with cast iron drains underneath. Scale builds on top while the bottom channels out. Because a heavy cutter can punch through weak iron, we use chain descalers and a lower-pressure jet, then check the wall thickness on camera.

    Scheduled Sewer Cleaning in Oklahoma City, OK

    History predicts the next backup. We set a cleaning interval. Homes with past root problems, older cast iron or a lot of kitchen grease do well on a cleaning every 12 to 24 months, timed before the growth or buildup closes the line.

    Root Masses in Oklahoma City Sewer Laterals

    Mature trees and old clay laterals do not mix. Roots find the joints. Our root cutting heads attach to the sectional cable and saw through the mass in passes, and a camera run afterward shows how much growth is left and where the roots are coming in.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Oklahoma City

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Oklahoma City

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. A cable only makes a path. Water at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes it all toward the street, which leaves the pipe close to its original diameter.
