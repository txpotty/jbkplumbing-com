    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Oklahoma City

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. There is no tank to refill.     Standard Toilet Installation in Oklahoma City

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. We shut off the stop, drain the tank and pull the old bowl. Once the old wax is scraped off, the flange gets checked for cracks, new stainless bolts go in, and the new bowl is lowered straight onto a fresh seal. The old one leaves with us.

    Smart Toilet and Bidet Seat Installation in Oklahoma City

    Bidet seats like the TOTO Washlet and Bio Bidet bolt onto most existing elongated bowls. A T-valve splits water off the fill line.     Comfort-Height and ADA Toilets in Oklahoma City
