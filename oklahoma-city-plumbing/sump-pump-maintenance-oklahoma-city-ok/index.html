    Sump Pump Maintenance in Oklahoma City, OK: What We Check

    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Oklahoma City

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. Our plumber drains the basin and digs the sediment out by hand. With the pit clean, the float swings without catching. The pump runs cooler too.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Oklahoma City

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. A backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or quits. Our plumber load tests the battery, tests the charger and alarm, then runs the backup alone with the primary unplugged. Most batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Oklahoma City, OK

    Grinding, clicking and a hum with no water moving each point to a different failure. We start with the outlet and the cord, then the float, then the motor. A tripped GFCI is a quick fix. A seized motor means a new pump, and we keep replacements on the truck so the pit is never left unprotected overnight.

    Float Switch Inspection in Oklahoma City, OK

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. Our plumber watches the float rise and drop through a complete cycle. Next we adjust the on and off levels so each run is longer and the starts are fewer.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Oklahoma City

    Zoeller pumps like the M53 and M63 use cast iron housings and an integral float switch, and they tend to outlast cheaper plastic units. Even so, we look for cracks in the casting and make sure the float moves freely. Common Zoeller parts ride on our trucks.

    Elevator Pit and Garage Pumps in Oklahoma City

    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out. Elevator pit pumps often need oil-detecting controls so hydraulic fluid never gets pumped out with the water. Duplex systems and high-water alarms get tested on a fixed schedule. The property manager gets a copy of every log.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Oklahoma City

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. We lift the unit out to look at the seals and the cord. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. The intake screen gets cleaned, and we look over the housing for corrosion, since pumps sitting in standing water for years rust from the bottom up.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Oklahoma City, OK

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, so the wear shows up in the shaft, the bearings and the float rod. We check the rod for bends and the stops for slipping. Bearings get a listen. A motor that whines or rattles on startup is usually close to done.
