    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Oklahoma City

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. Install time is shorter too. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Oklahoma City

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Oklahoma City, OK

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. Swapping a pump in an existing pit is the cheapest job, while a new pit means saw cutting, digging, gravel and a concrete patch, which roughly doubles the labor. Battery backups and alarms add to the total. Your written quote shows each option and its price.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Oklahoma City

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi models send a text too. Landlords like them. We add them to new installs and to pumps already in place.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Oklahoma City, OK

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary handles daily water. People with carpet downstairs buy these. They are worth it anywhere a few inches of water would mean new drywall.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Oklahoma City

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. The pit lid sits flush. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Oklahoma City, OK

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Oklahoma City, OK

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. The M53 and the M98 are the two we set most, depending on lift and inflow, and both come with mechanical float switches that hold up for years. Backup and combo units are available too. Pit size and lift decide which model we recommend.
