    T&P Valve Replacement in Oklahoma City

    Replacing a relief valve is a short job, but it has to be done right. We match the new valve to the heater's input rating. We drain the tank below the valve opening, thread in the new one with pipe dope rated for potable water, and reconnect a discharge pipe in copper, CPVC or another approved material. A capped discharge pipe is a serious hazard.

    Standard Tank Flushing in Oklahoma City

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. Hard water makes it more urgent. We drain, partly refill and drain again until the hose runs clear, sometimes four or five rounds. Then we close the valve and check that it reseals without a drip.

    Gas and Electric Tank Checks in Oklahoma City, OK

    Gas and electric tanks share the same steel shell, but the parts that wear out are different. Gas units get a burner, thermocouple and vent check. On electric units we meter both elements and check each thermostat terminal for scorching. Both get a flush.

    Yearly Maintenance Plans in Oklahoma City, OK

    A maintenance plan puts your tank on the calendar so nobody has to remember. We call you when it is due. Each plan is a fixed yearly rate for one or two visits, priority booking and a parts discount. Homes on well water or very hard water usually do better with the twice-a-year option.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Oklahoma City, OK

    The anode rod is a sacrificial metal rod that corrodes so the steel tank does not. Many rural homes rely on private wells, and some groundwater carries high mineral content that calls for softening or filtration. We loosen the hex head with a breaker bar and an impact gun, pull the rod and look at how much core wire is showing. Six inches of bare wire means replace it.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Costs in Oklahoma City, OK

    A yearly flush costs a small fraction of a new installed tank, which often runs $1,400 to $3,000 or more depending on size and venting. We tell you which one makes sense. A tank over 10 to 12 years old with rust at the base is a replacement. Younger tanks with a sound liner are worth maintaining.

    Emergency Tank Inspections in Oklahoma City

    Sudden changes call for a look the same day: a tank that suddenly rumbles, hot water that turns brown, a puddle forming under the pan, or a relief valve that will not stop dripping. Diagnosis comes first. Sometimes the fix is a flush and a new drain valve, and sometimes the tank is leaking from the liner and nothing will save it.

    Standard Tank Tune-Up in Oklahoma City
