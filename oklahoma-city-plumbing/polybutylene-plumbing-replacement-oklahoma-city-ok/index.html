    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Oklahoma City

    When a home with polybutylene pipes sits on a slab foundation, repiping becomes a more complex process. Our team specializes in full home repiping using PEX (cross-linked polyethylene), a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant material. PEX is an strong choice for Oklahoma homes, as it resists freezing better than rigid pipes and expands to handle temperature changes. We carefully plan the repipe to limit disruption, often running new lines through attics or existing wall cavities, delivering a complete and long-lasting upgrade for your entire plumbing system.

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Oklahoma City, OK

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. We use Uponor PEX-A with expansion fittings that do not restrict flow. New lines run from the main shutoff to every fixture, water heater and appliance. Most homes are completed in two to four days.

    Polybutylene to PEX Transition in Oklahoma City, OK

    Transitioning from polybutylene to PEX is a popular and cost-effective solution for many homeowners in Oklahoma City, OK. We use specialized transition fittings and techniques to cleanly connect your existing main water line to the new PEX system. This provides a watertight and code-compliant connection, providing a complete upgrade to a modern, durable plumbing material throughout your home's interior. This transition offers the benefits of PEX without the need for extensive excavation or disruption to your main water service line.
