    Residential Grease Interceptor Setup in Oklahoma City, OK

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Oklahoma City, OK

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, typically located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the complex excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, ensuring compliance with all local Oklahoma ordinances for waste management.

    The cost of grease trap installation in Oklahoma City, OK varies widely based on the type and size of the trap, whether it's an indoor or outdoor unit, and the complexity of the installation.     The lifespan of a grease trap depends on its type, material, and how well it's maintained. Steel grease traps may last 10 to 15 years, while durable fiberglass or polyethylene units can last 20 to 30 years or even longer. Regular cleaning and proper usage are key to lengthening its lifespan.
