    What a Plumbing Checkup Covers in Oklahoma City, OK

    Sewer and Drain Camera Inspection in Oklahoma City, OK

    A camera shows what the sewer looks like inside. Roots, sags, cracks and grease show up on camera, especially in old clay and cast iron. You get a copy of the video. We lay out options if something needs work.

    Hidden Leak Checks in Oklahoma City, OK

    A leak you cannot see still costs money. With fixtures off, we watch the meter and then use acoustic and thermal tools to find the source. Newer homes around Oklahoma City typically use PEX supply lines and PVC drains, while older ones may still have galvanized pipe that chokes flow. Early finds prevent mold. Most leaks we find are small and cheap to fix.

    Water Heater Flush and Inspection in Oklahoma City

    Popping or rumbling means sediment. Minerals settle on the tank bottom, trap heat and cause the popping you hear when it fires. Flushing clears it. Tankless units get a descaling flush through the service valves with a pump and descaler. Regular service helps keep the warranty valid.

    Sump Pump Testing in Oklahoma City, OK

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. Long droughts followed by heavy rain strain buried pipes as clay soil shrinks and then swells around them. We pour water into the pit to make sure the float lifts and the pump kicks on, check the discharge line for clogs or freezing risk, and inspect the check valve so water does not drain back in. Battery backups get load tested.

    Hot Water Tank Service in Oklahoma City, OK

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. On gas units we look at the burner flame, the flue draft and the gas connection for leaks, and on electric units we test the elements and the thermostats and check the wiring for heat damage. Both get flushed. Scale on the tank floor wastes energy and shortens tank life.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Oklahoma City

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. We pump descaler through the service valves. The inlet filter, venting and gas line get checked too.

    Gas Connector and Shutoff Checks in Oklahoma City

    Gas feeds water heaters, furnaces, ranges and dryers. Visible piping and connections get leak tested, shutoffs get turned, and old flex connectors get replaced. Repairs follow local code. If you ever smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility from outside.

    Softener Cleaning and Testing in Oklahoma City

    Hard water scale builds in pipes and appliances fast. We check the brine level, clean the injector screen, inspect the resin bed for fouling and confirm the regeneration cycle starts and finishes, then test the treated water hardness at a faucet. Fouled resin can be cleaned. Older resin may need replacing.
