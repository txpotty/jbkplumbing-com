    Sump Pump Maintenance in Oklahoma City, OK

    Severe thunderstorms and heavy spring rain can cause flash flooding in Oklahoma, overwhelming drainage and backing up sewers. A working sump pump is vital for homes with basements or crawl spaces when heavy rain overwhelms drainage. Our maintenance includes testing the float switch, checking for proper discharge, cleaning the pit of debris, and inspecting the check valve. That way your sump pump is ready to protect your home from flooding when unexpected storms hit Oklahoma City.

    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Oklahoma City, OK

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits significantly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to premature tank failure.

    Drainage Services and Hydrojet Plumbing in Oklahoma City

    Preventive drain maintenance is key to avoiding clogs and sewer backups. We offer routine drain camera inspections to identify grease buildup, roots, or other obstructions before they cause a problem. Our hydrojet plumbing service can blast away stubborn blockages, keeping your drainage system clear and free-flowing, which is especially important for homes with mature trees near the sewer line.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Oklahoma City

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers call for annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Oklahoma City

    Regular water heater maintenance is key to preventing sediment buildup. Many rural homes rely on private wells, and some groundwater carries high mineral content that calls for softening or filtration. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, improves efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.

    Water Softener Maintenance in Oklahoma City

    Water softeners combat hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance extends its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing expensive scale damage.
