    Repiping Services We Handle in Oklahoma City, OK

    PEX Repiping in Oklahoma City, OK

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. PEX shrugs off scale and chlorine, survives freezing better than copper and installs faster, so it is our most common repipe material. Our PEX-A installs use expansion fittings that do not shrink the flow path. That keeps pressure up at the far fixtures.

    Condo, Townhome and Multi-Family Repipes in Oklahoma City

    Shared walls and HOA rules make repiping attached homes more involved. Our office works with your HOA on approvals and shutoff times, and we take steps to protect the units next door. Building-wide jobs go in phases. For larger buildings, we repipe one stack or wing at a time so residents can stay in place.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Oklahoma City

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Both still turn up in older homes. We can usually identify it from the pipe at the water heater, and full replacement makes the house easier to insure and sell.

    Residential Repiping Plans in Oklahoma City

    Every house is piped a little differently, so a repipe starts with a walk-through. We count fixtures, check attic and crawlspace access, look at where the main comes in and ask about plans for future remodels before we draw a route. Then we size the lines. Bigger homes may need a 1-inch trunk so the shower does not drop when the dishwasher fills.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Oklahoma City, OK

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Flexible PEX soaks up some of the pressure spike behind water hammer, and its long fitting-free runs leave fewer weak points. Attic PEX gets kept away from flues and out of UV.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Oklahoma City, OK

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Rust-colored water in the morning, poor pressure upstairs and dripping threaded joints all point to failing galvanized. We replace it with PEX or copper. Most homeowners notice the pressure difference the first time they turn on the shower.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Oklahoma City, OK

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. The pipe gets brittle from the inside, and fittings crack, so a home that has had one polybutylene leak is usually at risk everywhere else in the system. Full replacement is the fix. Many insurers ask about it.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Oklahoma City

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. Type L tolerates UV, stays rigid and has decades of field history. It costs more. Our plumbers solder joints with lead-free solder or use press fittings, support the pipe at the spacing the code calls for and pressure test the system before any wall is closed.
