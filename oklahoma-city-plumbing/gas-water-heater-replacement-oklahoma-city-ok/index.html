    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Oklahoma City, OK Home

    No Hot Water Calls in Oklahoma City

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. We check those first. On a tank past ten years, a new heater usually beats a new valve.

    Bradford White Tank Replacement in Oklahoma City

    Most of the tanks on our trucks are Bradford White, partly because their Defender combustion system and ICON gas valve are simple to service. Model choice follows your hot water use. The install meets current code.

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Oklahoma City

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most Oklahoma homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Newer tanks can run taller than the old one. We check clearance before we order. Most of these swaps take two to three hours.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Replacement in Oklahoma City, OK

    Commercial gas heaters in restaurants and offices often run 75 to 100 gallons at 75,000 BTU or more, and they need a gas line sized for that load. Code is stricter here. Sizing comes from peak demand and recovery rate.

    Atmospheric Vent Upgrades in Oklahoma City, OK

    Flue pipes on older atmospheric heaters rust at the joints and lose their upward slope, and a loose connector can spill exhaust into the closet. We replace damaged sections and the draft hood if needed. We check draft with a match or mirror at the hood.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Oklahoma City

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. A second blower failure on an older tank is usually the point where a new heater makes more sense. We confirm a dedicated outlet first.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Oklahoma City

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. With a check or regulator on the main, expanding hot water has nowhere to go and the pressure spikes each heating cycle. Code commonly calls for an expansion tank. We set its air charge to match house pressure.

    Commercial Gas Tank Replacement in Oklahoma City

    Laundromats and multi-unit buildings work commercial tanks so hard that replacement comes on a predictable schedule rather than as a surprise. We plan the swap around your slow hours. Some jobs need added vent or gas work.
