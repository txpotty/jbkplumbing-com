    Same-Day Sink Replacement in Oklahoma City, OK

    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in Oklahoma City, OK

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in. A support frame goes into the cabinet and the sink is leveled before the countertop goes on. A deep bowl means a lower trap. The drain gets planned for that depth from day one.

    Apron-Front Sinks in Oklahoma City, OK

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the bowl must be set before the counter gets measured. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. That avoids an uneven reveal. Cast iron versions can top 150 pounds, so we bring two people.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Oklahoma City, OK

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and many are deep single bowls with the drain off center. We plan the trap around that offset drain so the disposal fits and the cabinet keeps usable space on one side. Faucet reach matters too.

    Drop-In Sink Replacement in Oklahoma City

    Pulling an old drop-in starts with cutting the caulk line and backing off the clips from below. Old clips are often rusted solid, so we bring a reciprocating saw and cut them off rather than prying on the counter. Laminate chips if you pry. The new sink needs to cover the old cutout fully.

    Kitchen Sink Installation Cost Factors in Oklahoma City, OK

    Undermount and farmhouse installs cost more than a drop-in swap. Each extra, like a new disposal, a moved drain, a lowered wall stub or replacing galvanized stubs, adds labor and shows up as its own line on the quote. The countertop is priced separately.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Oklahoma City

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and a lower gauge means thicker steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Top-mount and undermount plumb differently. Either way, we hook up the faucet, strainers and disposal.

    New Construction Sink Rough-In in Oklahoma City, OK

    Rough-in sets the drain height and the stops long before the cabinets arrive.
