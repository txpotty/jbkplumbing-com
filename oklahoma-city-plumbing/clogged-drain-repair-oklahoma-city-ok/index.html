    Hydro-Jetting Services in Oklahoma City, OK

    A jetter pushes water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and pulls itself down the pipe. It scours the whole wall. Cables punch a hole through a clog, but a jetter washes away the grease, scale and soft roots that cause the next clog to form. We use it on repeat offenders.

    Main Sewer Line Clog Repair in Oklahoma City

    When the toilet gurgles as the washer drains, the main line is usually the problem. Everything shares that pipe. We go in through the outside cleanout with a large sectional cable, then camera the line if the backup returns to check for roots or a sag. Hydro jetting handles the worst of it.

    Bathroom Sink and Vanity Drain Clogs in Oklahoma City, OK

    Vanity drains clog at the pop-up stopper first. The pivot rod catches hair. We pull and clean the stopper, clean the trap, cable the drain arm and swap out worn parts while we are there. Scale buildup is common there.

    Toilet Clog Repair in Oklahoma City

    A toilet clog usually sits in the trapway, the S-shaped channel inside the bowl. A plunger does not always reach. We use a closet auger with a rubber sleeve, which reaches through the trapway and pulls out the blockage without scratching the porcelain. We pull out a lot of wipes. If the auger finds nothing, the clog is past the toilet and we check the main.

    Main Sewer Line Clog Clearing in Oklahoma City, OK

    Several drains backing up at once usually means the clog is in the main. That is a bigger job.
