    Jammed Disposal Repair in Oklahoma City

    Jams usually trace back to one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. The reset button may need a press.     Leaking Disposal Fix in Oklahoma City, OK

    When the drip starts at the top, the putty under the sink flange is usually dried out. A leak on the side is usually the discharge gasket or a loose hose clamp.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Oklahoma City, OK

    There are four usual leak points on a disposal: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Three of the four are fixable. A leak from the housing means it is time for a new unit.

    A disposal that hums but will not grind is getting power but is stuck, while one that is silent has no power at all. The two need different fixes.
