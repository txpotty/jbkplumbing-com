    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Oklahoma City, OK

    For high-hazard applications in Oklahoma City, OK where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is usually required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, using a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. JBK Plumbing is experienced with RPZ installation, making sure all components are correctly sized, positioned and set up according to state and local plumbing codes. Proper installation is critical for effective operation.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Oklahoma City

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a common backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Oklahoma City, DCVAs are frequently installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers carefully install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Oklahoma City, OK
