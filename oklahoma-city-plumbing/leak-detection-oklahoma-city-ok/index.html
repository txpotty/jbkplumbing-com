    Commercial Building Leaks in Oklahoma City, OK

    Pool Leak Detection in Oklahoma City

    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation probably has a leak. We start with a bucket test. Then we pressure test the suction and return lines, listen for leaks with a hydrophone underwater, and dye test around the skimmer, lights and main drain so we can point to the exact fitting or crack.

    Slab Leak Detection in Oklahoma City

    Concrete slab homes have supply lines buried in sand below the floor, and a pinhole there can run for months before anyone notices. Look for warm tile first. We pressure test the hot and cold sides separately, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak through the concrete and confirm with a thermal camera on hot water lines.

    Preventive Leak Inspections in Oklahoma City, OK

    Checking for leaks once a year catches the small stuff. We test static pressure, read the meter with everything off and look at supply lines, water heater fittings and shutoff valves. Older homes with galvanized pipe or original water heater connectors benefit most from a yearly check, because those parts often fail together. Short visit, big savings.

    Hidden Wall and Ceiling Leaks in Oklahoma City

    Bubbling paint, soft drywall and a musty smell are the signs we hear about most. All of them mean water. Thermal cameras find the wet area, and a moisture meter confirms it. Acoustic tools help when the leak is on a pressurized line, and once the source is pinned down we cut a single neat access opening instead of chasing the stain across the wall.

    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Oklahoma City, OK

    On a ground microphone, copper leaks are easy to hear. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. Both show up on our schedule every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX top the list of what we find.
