    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Oklahoma City, OK

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. That means the heater can go in a spot with no chimney or vertical flue, and the vent can run sideways out an exterior wall as long as it stays within the length limit. They need an outlet. Termination clearances follow the manual.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Oklahoma City

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They cost the least, but the vent has to be the right size, slope upward and keep its clearances. We inspect the existing vent. We replace bad sections.

    Gas Water Heater Permit and Inspection in Oklahoma City

    Gas water heater installs usually need a permit and a final inspection. Permits are generally required for water heater installs, gas line work, sewer repairs and remodels that move fixtures.     High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Oklahoma City, OK

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Condensing units vent through PVC and need a condensate line, so placement and install work differ from a standard tank. Rebates may apply. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.
