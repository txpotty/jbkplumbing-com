    Hydro-Jetting Services in Oklahoma City, OK

    Jetting puts water through a nozzle at 3,000 to 4,000 psi and scrubs the pipe wall clean. Grease, scale and fine roots that a cable rides over come off with the water.     Rooting vs. Pipe Repair in Oklahoma City, OK

    Rooting makes sense when the pipe is sound and just clogged. When roots return in months, or the cable hangs on a broken joint every time, repairing that section starts to save money.
