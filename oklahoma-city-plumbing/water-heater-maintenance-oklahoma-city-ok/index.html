    Anode Rod Checks in Oklahoma City

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. It is a long metal rod screwed into the top of the tank, and once it has corroded down to bare wire, the water starts eating the steel tank lining instead. We pull it and look. A new anode costs far less than a new tank.

    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in Oklahoma City

    A blue flame is what we want to see. Poor flames mean dirty burners, so we clean them, test the sensor and check the draft. Bad venting is dangerous. We test the gas connection too.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Oklahoma City

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment buries the lower element until it overheats and fails, leaving you with a short supply of hot water. We test both elements. Thermostats and wiring get checked with the power off.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Oklahoma City, OK

    Tankless units need descaling. Mineral scale coats the inside of the heat exchanger, restricting flow and making the unit work harder, and eventually it starts throwing error codes or cutting out in the middle of a shower. A pump circulates descaler through it. We also check the igniter, the fan and the exhaust vent for safe operation.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Oklahoma City
