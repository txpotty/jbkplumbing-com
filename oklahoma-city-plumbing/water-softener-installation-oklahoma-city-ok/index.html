    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Oklahoma City

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. Each install follows the brand's specs for drain, bypass and power, then we program it to your water test. Connections and setup included. A loop saves time.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Oklahoma City

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Soft water reaches every fixture. We can skip the hose bibs.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Oklahoma City, OK

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. Taste improves fast. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    Water Softener Replacement in Oklahoma City, OK

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. We remove the old unit and install a new metered model, then check the loop and drain line while everything is apart. Salt use drops. You will notice soft water the same day.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Oklahoma City

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. Placing it on the main means the water heater, the dishwasher and every fixture get soft water, not just the kitchen. Sizing comes first. Your test result and water use decide the tank size.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Oklahoma City, OK

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first. Commercial softeners we install use larger resin tanks or twin-tank alternating systems so soft water is available around the clock, even during regeneration. Dishwashers and boilers last longer. We schedule around your hours.

    Softener Loop Installation in Oklahoma City

    No loop? A loop is the plumbing that routes the main line through a softener, and many older homes were built without one. We cut it in for you. Outdoor taps can stay on hard water, which saves salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Oklahoma City

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. Pairs well with a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.
