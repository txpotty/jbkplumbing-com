    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Oklahoma City, OK

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a quick spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades. We show you the condition of the pipe we expose so you can make the call with actual information.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Oklahoma City

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a especially challenging issue. Homes built from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s may have polybutylene supply lines, which are known for sudden leaks. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to major structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to locate slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak promptly in Oklahoma City is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Oklahoma City, OK

    A full break can flood a yard, wash out soil under walkways and leave your home without water. Our emergency crews shut off the supply, find the break and set up a temporary bypass when the repair will take more than a few hours. Most emergency breaks are repaired the same day. We also document the damage with photos for your insurance or HOA.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Oklahoma City, OK

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can work loose those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is in charge of the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve in the same visit.

    Underground Leak Detection in Oklahoma City, OK

    Many water line leaks occur underground, making them hard to detect without specialized equipment. Our technicians use modern acoustic and thermal imaging tools to accurately locate hidden leaks without unnecessary digging. Early detection saves you money on water bills and prevents costly damage to your foundation. Soil movement can crack sewer lines and water lines over time, so foundation shifts and plumbing problems often go together. Once located, we provide a clear repair plan, so the problem is resolved efficiently for your property in Oklahoma City.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Oklahoma City

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off fast in an emergency.

    Many older homes in Oklahoma City, OK still have galvanized steel water lines, which are prone to corrosion, rust, and reduced water flow over time. While replacement is often the best long-term solution, we can provide targeted repairs for individual leaks in galvanized pipes where practical.
