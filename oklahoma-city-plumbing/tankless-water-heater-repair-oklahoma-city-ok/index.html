    What Our Tankless Plumbers Fix in Oklahoma City, OK

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Repair in Oklahoma City, OK

    Rinnai units are common, and scale causes most of their service calls. The LC code is the unit's own warning that the exchanger needs a flush. We also see dirty flame rods behind a code 11 or 12 and tired combustion fans behind a code 61, and each part gets tested against Rinnai's published values. Parts come from the factory list.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Oklahoma City

    Rinnai shows an LC code and Navien posts a scale warning when the exchanger runs hot from mineral buildup. Resetting the code without a flush only buys a few days, because the scale is still in there. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves, circulate descaling solution through the exchanger for close to an hour, then flush and clean the inlet screen. Yearly is right for most hard-water homes.

    Tankless Repair vs Replacement Cost in Oklahoma City, OK

    Descaling usually runs $175 to $350, and sensor or igniter repairs land between $250 and $600. A heat exchanger out of warranty can cost $800 to $1,500 in parts and labor. Replacement is a bigger number. Installed, a new gas tankless typically runs $3,000 to $5,500, so on a unit past 15 years with a failed exchanger we usually lean toward replacing.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Repair in Oklahoma City, OK

    Noritz heaters hold up well, though flow sensors and vent joints still fail with age. A code 90 points to combustion trouble, often a dirty fan or a restricted intake. We clean the fan and burner, check the vent joints for gaps, and test the flow sensor's output against the spec sheet. Gaskets get replaced if they leak.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Oklahoma City, OK

    Condensing tankless heaters pull extra heat out of the exhaust and make acidic condensate that has to drain somewhere. That condensate runs through a trap and, where code or the manual calls for it, a neutralizer filled with limestone or marble chips before it reaches the drain. A plugged trap will lock the heater out. We clean the trap, refill the neutralizer and check the PVC vent for sag or the wrong slope.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Repair in Oklahoma City, OK

    Whole-house electric tankless units pull a lot of current, often 100 to 150 amps on two or three dedicated breakers. When a breaker trips under load, the cause can be a shorted element or a loose, scorched lug, and both get tested with a meter before anything is replaced. Element resistance and supply voltage get measured. Scorched wire gets cut back.

    Electric Tankless Element Repair in Oklahoma City, OK

    Electric units fail in their own way. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart models use two to four heating elements in separate chambers, so a single burned element leaves the heater behind at high flow, while a failed flow switch means no heat at all. We check each element's resistance, read voltage under load and clamp amperage on each leg, then confirm the panel and wire size match the unit's rating.

    Same-Day Tankless Repair in Oklahoma City

    Water dripping from inside the cabinet, or a fault you cannot reset, should be seen that day. We bring sensors, igniters, flame rods and filters, so a good share of visits end with hot water. Heat exchangers are different. We check the warranty, order the part, and shut the water at the service valves so the cabinet stays dry until it arrives.
