    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Oklahoma City

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to recurring clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it will hold.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Oklahoma City, OK

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Oklahoma City.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Repair in Oklahoma City

    A single spot repair typically runs $1,500 to $4,500 depending on depth and access. Trenchless lining usually costs $80 to $250 per foot, and full replacement of a typical residential line ranges from $5,000 to $15,000. We give you a written quote after the camera inspection is done.

    Many cities require a permit for sewer line replacement and for any work in the public right-of-way. Small repairs on private property may not, depending on local rules. We check with your city and pull the permit when it is required.

    Common signs include recurring drain clogs, multiple fixtures backing up, foul odors coming from drains or the yard, gurgling noises from toilets, unusually lush patches of grass in your yard or dips in your lawn. If you notice any of these, especially combined with slow drains, it's wise to call for a professional inspection promptly.

    Most spot repairs are completed in one day, including excavation and backfill. Larger replacements or jobs that need a city right-of-way permit can take two to four days. We give you a timeline before we start.

    Trenchless sewer repair is a method of repairing or replacing sewer lines with minimal digging, often requiring only one or two access points. It involves techniques like pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, or pipe lining, where a resin-impregnated liner is inserted and cured inside the existing pipe. This protects your landscaping and is less disruptive than traditional methods.

    For sewer line replacements, we primarily use durable, code-compliant materials such as PVC for its longevity and resistance to corrosion, or HDPE for trenchless applications. We select the best material based on your needs and local soil conditions.

    Watch for multiple drains backing up at once, gurgling toilets, sewage smells in the yard and wet or unusually green patches over the line. Water backing up into a tub or shower when you run the washing machine is another classic warning sign.

    We use Schedule 40 PVC or ABS that meets local code, joined with approved fittings and shielded no-hub couplings at transitions. For lining, we use epoxy-saturated liners rated for decades of service.
