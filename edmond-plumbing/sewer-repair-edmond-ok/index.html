    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Edmond

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. The crack lets soil in and sewage out.     Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Edmond, OK

    Replacement makes sense when the camera shows failures every few feet. Patching that line is wasted money.     Sewer Line Leak Detection and Repair in Edmond, OK

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. A lush stripe over the line is another clue.     Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Edmond

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. Over decades it goes oval and blisters. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, and once the camera shows failures along the whole run, patching it stops being worth the money. PVC goes in, usually end to end. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Edmond

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease is the usual culprit. We also install two-way cleanouts for future maintenance.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Edmond

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. The pipe was leaking first. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they thicken until they crack the pipe or separate the joint completely. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. Lining or a joint replacement keeps them from coming back.

    After-Hours Sewer Repair in Edmond

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Edmond, OK

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Two access holes, sometimes one.
