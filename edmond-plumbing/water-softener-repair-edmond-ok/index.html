    Resin Bed Replacement & Regeneration Issues in Edmond

    Over time, the resin beads inside your water softener can become fouled, damaged, or depleted, reducing their ability to remove hardness minerals. If your system is regenerating but still delivering hard water, the resin bed may be the culprit. We can test the resin, flush the system to remove iron or sediment buildup, or perform a complete resin replacement. This process restores your softener's core function, ensuring effective hard water treatment for years to come.

    Water Softener Leak Detection & Repair in Edmond, OK

    Leaks around your water softener can be caused by cracked tanks, loose fittings, faulty O-rings, or pressure issues. Even a small leak can lead to water damage and wasted water.     Control Valve Repair in Edmond

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, directing the flow of water and starting regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water or waste salt. JBK Plumbing technicians are experts at diagnosing and repairing common control valve issues, whether mechanical or electronic, restoring precise timing and efficient operation. We stock common replacement parts to speed up your repair in Edmond.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Edmond

    A frequent issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to poor regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and making sure the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering targeted repairs or replacements.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Edmond, OK

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, regulating the flow of water and initiating regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water, regenerate too often, or leak. We diagnose issues like stuck pistons, worn out seals, motor failures, or programming errors. Our plumbers carry a range of replacement parts for popular brands like Fleck, Clack, and Autotrol, allowing a quick and effective repair to restore proper operation.
