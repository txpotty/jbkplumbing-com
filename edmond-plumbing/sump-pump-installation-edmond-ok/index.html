    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Edmond, OK

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Edmond, OK

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. Testing is done with a full pit.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Edmond, OK

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Edmond

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. Install time is shorter too. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Edmond

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. For those areas we set an exterior basin with a submersible pump and run the discharge to a safe point, often a pop-up emitter in the yard. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause. We keep the outlet away from your foundation and the neighbor line.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Edmond, OK

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install their M53 and M98 models often. Backup and combo units are available too. Pit size and lift decide which model we recommend.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Edmond, OK

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. We set a basin at the lowest point, run perforated drain pipe to it where needed and seal the vapor barrier around the lid so the space stays dry. Clearance is usually under two feet. We route the discharge out through the rim joist and away from the foundation.
