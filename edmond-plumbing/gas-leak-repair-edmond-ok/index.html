    Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Edmond, OK

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. Bonding means a 6 AWG copper wire from the gas piping to the electrical grounding system. Plenty of older installs lack it. Repairs use the same brand's fittings as the existing tubing, since manufacturers do not allow mixing parts. A splice that skips that rule fails the inspection.

    Underground Gas Line Repair in Edmond

    Yard lines run from the meter to the house, and often on to a detached garage, a pool heater, a grill or an outdoor kitchen. Steel pipe in the ground corrodes wherever the wrap was nicked during the original install. Roots and settling do damage too. We call 811 for locates, dig at the suspected spot and replace the bad run with yellow polyethylene pipe and tracer wire. Sand goes around the new pipe before backfill, per Edmond trench requirements.

    Residential Gas Leak Detection in Edmond

    A gas leak in a house rarely sits where the smell is strongest, because gas drifts along joists and collects in closets and basements. We start with a gauge on the system to confirm there is a leak at all. Then the sniffer comes out. A combustible gas detector follows the reading from fitting to fitting until it spikes. Bubble solution on the suspect joint gives the final confirmation.

    Commercial Gas Leak Repair in Edmond
