    Installing a main is more than digging a trench. You need a permit, code knowledge and the right meter connections, and a joint that is not made right can leak underground for months while the water bill climbs and the soil around your foundation soaks. A licensed plumber is the safe bet.

    PEX is flexible, cheaper, and has fewer fittings, so there are fewer places for a leak to start. Copper costs more but has decades of history behind it, and it handles heat and pressure well, so the right choice depends on your budget and the water chemistry in Edmond. Either one meets code.

    PEX and copper are the usual picks, with HDPE on long runs. Many rural homes rely on private wells, and some groundwater carries high mineral content that calls for softening or filtration. The deciding factors are usually cost and water chemistry.
