    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds.     Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. Every salt-based install includes a sized resin tank, a brine tank, a bypass and a drain to an air gap. Then we program it. We set regeneration overnight.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Muskogee

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Maintenance is light. They work best on moderate water.
