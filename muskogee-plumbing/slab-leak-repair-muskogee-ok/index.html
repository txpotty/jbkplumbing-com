    Copper and PEX fail in different ways under a slab. Copper pinholes.     Slab Leak Detection in Muskogee

    The first test is the water meter. Every fixture gets shut off. A meter that keeps turning tells us to isolate hot from cold, test each side and walk the losing line with a ground mic. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor.

    Ground Radar and Acoustic Locating in Muskogee

    Ground-penetrating radar shows what is inside the slab before we cut. Radar maps steel in the concrete. Combined with a listening device, radar finds leaks in any pipe type, even with traffic noise, since radar maps the route and the mic confirms the spot. We use both in Muskogee.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Muskogee

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. The bill gives them away. Look for a rising bill, a meter that spins with nothing on or damp carpet, since a cold leak wets the soil as much as a hot one. Your Muskogee home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and repair or reroute it.
