    Slab Leak Reroute in Enid

    Older copper under a slab tends to leak again. A reroute stops the cycle. We leave the leaking run in the ground and pull new PEX through the attic and down the walls, cutting openings where they are easiest to patch, often behind a vanity or in a closet.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Enid, OK

    Two or three slab leaks in a few years point to the pipe, not bad luck. That is when a repipe pays off. PEX bends with the structure, won't corrode and runs overhead, so a whole-house repipe leaves nothing under the slab. It suits many Enid homes.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Enid

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. Watch the bill instead. Look for a rising bill, a meter that spins with nothing on or damp carpet, since a cold leak wets the soil as much as a hot one. Your Enid home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and fix it or run a new one.
