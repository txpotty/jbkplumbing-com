    Well Pump Diagnostic Services in Enid, OK

    Voltage, amperage, pressure and flow rate tell us most of what is happening down the well without pulling a single length of pipe. A weak motor draws high amps.     Submersible Well Pump Repair in Enid

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. No water, low pressure or a pump that hums without moving water are the usual signs one has failed. We test the motor from the surface first. If it has to come up, a pump hoist lifts the pipe safely, and we replace the burned motor, worn impellers or a bad check valve before setting it back.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Enid, OK

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. We work on Franklin motors, QD control boxes and SubDrive units regularly. We carry the common Franklin parts. A motor that tests bad on a megohm meter usually gets replaced rather than rewound, since a new motor costs less than shipping and rebuilding the old one.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Enid, OK

    Electrical faults cause a big share of well calls, from a tripped breaker to a burned contact in the pressure switch. After that, we look at the capacitor and relay in the control box. We lock out the power before testing anything. All wiring and splices meet Oklahoma electrical code before the pump restarts.

    Deep Well Pump Repair in Enid

    Deep wells get submersible pumps. They push water up, run quietly and last, but motors, impellers and splices still wear out. After pulling the pump, we test each part and replace only the failed one. Heat-shrink splice kits and a torque arrestor protect the new work in your Enid, OK well.

    Low Water Pressure Diagnosis in Enid

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. Common causes include a plugged sediment filter, a low switch setting or a yard line leak. In some cases the pump is fine and the well just cannot keep up with demand. We test each one.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Enid

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. A waterlogged tank or a ruptured bladder causes most of it. We check the precharge with the tank drained. Set it 2 psi under cut-in, which means about 38 psi for a 40/60 switch.

    Jet Pump Repair in Enid, OK

    Shallow wells and booster systems often use jet pumps. Lost prime, seal leaks and impeller wear account for most jet pump calls. Our crew in Enid restores prime, replaces worn parts and checks the suction line for air leaks. Where water levels swing through the year, a falling level can starve the pump and cost it prime.
