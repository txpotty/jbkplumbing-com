    Why Choose JBK Plumbing for Grease Trap Cleaning in Enid, OK?

    Skipped pump-outs usually announce themselves at the floor drain first, with gray water pooling around the grate after the dish machine drains. The smell shows up next. That sour, rotten odor is grease breaking down and releasing hydrogen sulfide gas. The same gas corrodes concrete, which shortens the life of an in-ground interceptor.

    • Odor Control, A pumped trap stops the sour, rotten-egg smell that decaying grease gives off.
    • Extended Lifespan, Less hydrogen sulfide in the tank means slower corrosion of concrete and baffles.
    • Prevent Backups, Pumping on schedule keeps grease from leaving the trap and hardening in the lateral.
