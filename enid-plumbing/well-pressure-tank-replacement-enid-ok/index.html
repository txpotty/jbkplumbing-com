    Pressure Switch and Gauge Integration in Enid, OK

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Switch and tank are a team. A switch set at 40/60 needs a tank pre-charged to 38 psi, and a gauge that reads correctly so you can see what the system is doing, and a cheap new gauge beats guessing. Small parts, big role.

    Emergency Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Enid

    A tank that bursts or a pump that runs nonstop needs attention fast. Shut off the breaker to the pump. A pump that keeps running against a failed system can overheat and burn out, so we isolate the problem and get a new tank in, often the same day. Water back tonight.

    Pressure Tanks for Well Houses and Barns in Enid, OK

    Tanks in outbuildings face heat, cold and damp. Steel rusts fast in a damp well house. We often recommend a composite tank in those spaces, raise it off the floor on a stand, and insulate exposed piping and the switch when the building sees freezing weather. Simple protection.

    Diaphragm Pressure Tank Replacement in Enid, OK

    Diaphragm tanks use a flexible sheet clamped inside the shell. The diaphragm splits air from water. When it fails, symptoms match a bladder tank, with short cycling, pressure swings and water spitting from the air valve. Replacement is the usual answer.

    Residential Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Enid

    Most homes on a well have a tank between 20 and 86 gallons sitting near the pressure switch. First step, kill the breaker to the pump.     Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Enid

    Flex-Lite tanks have a composite shell. Rust is not a concern. That makes them a good pick for damp basements, crawlspaces and well houses where steel tanks tend to corrode from the bottom up, and they are lighter to carry into tight spaces. Same plumbing as steel.
