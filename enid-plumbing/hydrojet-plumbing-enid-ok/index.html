    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Enid

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. A cable cuts a channel through it. Jetting with a spinning nozzle strips the grease from the full circumference so it flushes downstream instead of re-forming a plug at the next fitting.

    Commercial Hydrojet Plumbing in Enid, OK

    Businesses put more through their drains than any house. Multifamily and office buildings carry heavy daily loads. We jet stacks, laterals and mains for commercial buildings, often before opening hours, and we leave a written report with camera notes for the property manager's records.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Enid, OK

    Older cast iron drains rust and scale from the inside, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. A jet clears that crust. Badly corroded cast iron with thin walls or cracks needs gentler settings or no jetting at all, and the camera tells us which before we start, along with whether the pipe is a candidate for lining.

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Enid

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. We camera every line before we set up the jetter. The video shows the pipe material, the condition of each joint, and any cracks, offsets or sags, which tells us the nozzle and pressure to use or whether we should not jet that line at all.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Enid

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. We jet the full main to the street tap. Four-inch mains get a heavy nozzle and high flow to push debris downstream.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Enid, OK

    Restaurant kitchen lines carry hot grease that hardens as it cools. Much of it coats the pipe before it ever reaches the interceptor. Short jetting passes keep loosened grease from plugging the line downstream. Quarterly works for most.

    Residential Hydrojetting in Enid
