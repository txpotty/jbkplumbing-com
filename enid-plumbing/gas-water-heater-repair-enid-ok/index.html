    Burner Assembly and Venting System Repair in Enid

    A worn burner assembly can result in insufficient heating or unusual noises. We carefully inspect the burner for blockages, corrosion or wear.     Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Enid

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures often involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the failure. Correct vent slope and termination are checked before we leave.

    Thermostat & Gas Valve Repair in Enid, OK

    If your gas water heater isn't heating water to the correct temperature, or if it is running too hot, the thermostat or gas valve might be to blame. These components regulate the gas supply and temperature. Our plumbers in Enid, OK can test the thermostat for accuracy and inspect the gas valve for any blockages or faults. We carry a range of replacement parts for various brands, allowing for fast and efficient repairs.

    Tank Leaks & Pressure Problems in Enid

    Even gas water heaters can develop tank leaks, often due to corrosion from hard water or high water pressure. Much of Oklahoma has moderately hard to very hard water, especially in western areas, which leaves mineral deposits in water heaters and fixtures. While some leaks may call for a replacement, minor leaks or pressure relief valve issues can often be repaired. Our team checks the extent of the damage, inspects the anode rod and addresses pressure imbalances to prevent further problems in your Enid home.

    Pilot Light Repair in Enid

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Enid, OK

    The gas control valve regulates temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something goes wrong. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or approved universal model. We leak test and confirm temperature control afterward.

    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Enid

    Strange noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem. Hard water can speed up sediment accumulation. Our repair service includes flushing the tank and inspecting for sediment, addressing pressure issues or checking other components that might be causing the noise, improving efficiency and quiet operation.
