    Gas Tankless Water Heater Repair in Enid

    Pressure at full fire matters on a gas tankless burner, not just when it first fires. We put a manometer on the inlet test port and run every hot tap in the house, because pressure that drops below the manufacturer's minimum under load causes flame loss and cold slugs.     Tankless Error Code Diagnostics in Enid, OK

    Every tankless brand flashes its own codes, so a code 11 on one unit means something else on another. A Rinnai 11 is no ignition, a Navien E003 is ignition failure, and a Noritz 11 is also an ignition fault, but the tests behind them differ. We read the code, pull the history and test the part tied to it. Guessing gets expensive.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Enid

    Rinnai shows an LC code and Navien posts a scale warning when the exchanger runs hot from mineral buildup.     Igniter and Flame Rod Repair in Enid, OK

    Gas tankless heaters light with a hot surface or spark igniter, then prove flame with a flame rod. An oxidized flame rod gives a weak microamp reading and the board shuts the gas valve after a few seconds. We clean or replace the rod, look for a cracked igniter or a lazy spark, and confirm the gas valve opens on call. Five-minute fix sometimes.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Repair in Enid

We clear the trap, check the PVC vent pitch and test the circulation pump on NPE-A models with built-in recirculation. Then we fire it at low and high flow.
