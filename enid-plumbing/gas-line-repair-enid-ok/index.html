    What Our Gas Line Plumbers Fix in Enid, OK

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Enid

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. New coated stainless connectors go on, sized to the appliance, with a reachable shutoff valve if the old one is stuck or missing. This is a quick fix. Furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers each need a sound connection, and most need a drip leg before the appliance valve.

    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Enid

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. Our plumbers reseal or replace the leaking fittings and sections using new pipe and approved sealant. Rusted pipe goes too. Heavily rusted pipe in garages, crawlspaces or outside walls is replaced before it fails, and we pressure test the full system afterward.

    Gas Appliance Line Installation in Enid, OK

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners. We install the branch line, add an accessible shutoff valve and a sediment trap where the code calls for one, then connect the appliance with a new coated flexible connector. If the existing appliance line is leaking or damaged, we fix it on the same visit.

    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Enid, OK

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. Once the coating cracks, the pipe starts to corrode, and wet soil from sprinklers or a low spot in the yard speeds that up a great deal. We call 811 before digging. We lay polyethylene with tracer wire or coated steel where exposed, then backfill and clean up after the test passes.

    Gas Permits and Inspections in Enid

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from Enid building officials. We pull the permit, book the inspection and meet the inspector at the gauge so you do not have to. Inspection usually happens within a few days. After it passes, the gas company can restore service, and we can return to relight the pilots.

    Emergency Gas Line Repair in Enid

    If you smell gas, leave the building, skip the light switches and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Then call us.     Propane Gas Line Service in Enid, OK

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.
