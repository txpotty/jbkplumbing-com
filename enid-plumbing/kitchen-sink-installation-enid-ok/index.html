    Undermount Sink Installation in Enid, OK

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, with silicone run around the rim.     Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Enid

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for tile, laminate and butcher block counters. It is often the quickest swap. We check the cutout size, seal the rim and tighten the clips evenly, and in most rentals and older homes the job is done in a couple of hours.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Enid, OK

    Every fabricator handles undermount clips a bit differently. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. Either way, the bowl gets braced. We do not connect the trap until the adhesive has cured.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Enid

    Both bowls of a double sink share one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. The layout depends on your disposal and dishwasher, because the disposal side changes where the tee sits and how much slope each bowl gets. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Enid, OK

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and most are single deep bowls with an offset drain. We plan the trap around that offset drain so the disposal fits and the cabinet keeps usable space on one side. Faucet reach matters too.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Enid

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. The bowl is mounted, the rim sealed with silicone and the sink braced while the adhesive sets, normally overnight before it can take a full bowl of water. Strainers and traps go in after.
