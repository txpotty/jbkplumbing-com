    What Our Commercial Plumbers Do in Enid, OK

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Enid

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Soil movement can separate sewer lines and water lines over time, so foundation shifts and plumbing problems often go together. Meter and pressure tests confirm it, and acoustic gear plus thermal imaging narrow the spot down to a foot or so. Then we decide. Some leaks are best fixed with a small cut through the slab, while others are cheaper to abandon and reroute overhead. We price both so you can compare.

    Emergency Commercial Plumbing in Enid

    A burst supply line above a drop ceiling can soak inventory and ceiling tile in under an hour. Shut the main first. We can talk your staff through the shutoff while the plumber drives over, then isolate the broken branch and get the rest of the building back on water. Backups get handled the same way. After clearing the line we run a camera so you know whether it is a one-time clog or damaged pipe.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Enid

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. We use high-pressure jetting with rotating nozzles that scrub grease off the whole circumference of 4-inch and 6-inch lines, not just the bottom. Jetting keeps the line open for months instead of days. We see the most benefit in restaurants, schools and multi-family stacks.

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Businesses in Enid

    A camera inspection shows the inside of a drain line on a monitor and records it so you can share it with a landlord or contractor. Guesswork goes away. We push the camera from a cleanout, note the footage at every break, root mass or sag, and use a locator to mark depth and position on the ground above. The video is yours to keep. That footage helps you budget repairs and explain them to owners or a board.

    Grease Trap Installation in Enid

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Small kitchens can get by with an under-sink trap, but bigger operations often need an in-ground interceptor of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We size the unit from the equipment schedule, set it at the right grade and vent it properly so it passes inspection the first time.

    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in Enid, OK

Wall-hung toilets that keep clogging or rocking get a carrier check, since a loose bowl can leak inside the wall. Low pressure on one fixture usually points to a clogged supply stop.
