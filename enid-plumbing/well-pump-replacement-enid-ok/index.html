    What We Fix on Well Systems in Enid, OK

    No-Water Calls in Enid

    Pump trouble rarely gives notice. One morning the faucet coughs air and then nothing, and by then the motor has often been struggling for weeks. We test the control box and pressure switch before pulling anything.

    Drop Pipe and Wire Replacement in Enid

    Galvanized drop pipe from the 1970s and 1980s rusts from the inside, and a pinhole at 150 feet will bleed pressure every time the pump shuts off. We replace it with schedule 120 PVC or poly pipe, depending on the depth and the pump weight. Wire gets the same look. Cracked insulation and taped splices get cut out.

    Grundfos Well Pump Replacement in Enid, OK

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have a built-in soft start and dry-run protection, and they run on two wires with no control box at the surface. They cost more up front. Add the CU 301 controller to an SQE and you get constant pressure.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in Enid

    Irrigation and stock wells often run 3 to 10 HP pumps, sometimes on three-phase power with a motor starter and overload protection at the panel. We replace those pumps and the starters that protect them. We schedule around watering times.

    Constant Pressure Pump Systems in Enid

    Franklin SubDrive and Grundfos SQE are the two constant pressure systems we install most. They get by with a much smaller tank because the drive matches pump speed to demand. Soft starts also cut wear on the motor. Expect to pay more up front.

    Jet Pump Suction Line Repair in Enid, OK

    Foot valves and suction lines cause most jet pump trouble, not the pump. A pinhole in the suction pipe lets air in, the pump loses prime, and it runs dry until the seal burns up. We pressure test the line. A well that is too deep for suction needs a submersible, and we will quote that instead.

    Well Pump Brands We Install in Enid, OK

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Sta-Rite cover nearly every well pump we install. Each brand has strengths, and we choose based on your well. Parts for Goulds and Franklin are easy to get, which matters when a control box dies on a weekend.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Enid, OK

    No-water calls often end at the control box, not the pump. On a three-wire submersible, the start capacitor or the relay in that box fails far more often than the motor down the well does. We test them first. A $60 part beats a $2,500 pump pull.
