    Grease Trap Brands We Install in Enid

    Commercial Grease Trap Installation in Enid

    A commercial kitchen with a three-compartment sink, a pre-rinse station and two floor sinks can push 50 gallons a minute of greasy water at the dinner rush. Our sizing starts from your peak flow, the worst ten minutes of the night, rather than a daily average. Undersized traps are the usual failure. We set the unit, run the inlet and outlet in schedule 40 PVC or cast iron, and add the flow control fitting the manufacturer calls for.

    Replacement and Upgrade Services in Enid, OK

    Steel traps from decades back rust through at the baffle seams, and by then the outlet is usually passing grease straight to the sewer. We run a camera down the outlet first to see how much grease got past the old unit. Sometimes the line needs jetting too. If your seats or menu grew since opening, the swap is the time to step up to a bigger GPM rating.

    Under-Sink Grease Trap Setup in Enid, OK

    Small cafes and coffee bars often get by with a 10 to 25 GPM hydromechanical trap tucked under the prep sink or beside it. The unit goes on the floor or in a shallow recess, and the flow control fitting goes upstream. Clearance matters most. Your staff needs to lift the lid and clean it weekly, so we leave space to do that without unbolting anything.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Enid

    Inside a hydromechanical interceptor, baffles do the separating while a flow control fitting with an air intake holds incoming water to the rated flow. We pipe the air intake the way the manufacturer and the code both require. Get that wrong and the trap siphons. We also set the unit level within a quarter inch, since a tilted trap short-circuits the baffles and loses capacity.

    Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in Enid, OK

    Sizing starts with fixture drain rates and ends with where the trap can physically go. A trap 40 feet from the sink lets grease cool and stick in the pipe before it ever reaches the unit. Closer is better. We also check that a pump truck can reach an outdoor tank with about 100 feet of hose, since a tank the hauler cannot reach costs you more on every service.

    Restaurant Grease Trap Compliance in Enid, OK

    Most sewer authorities run a FOG program with an inspector who opens your trap and checks your pump-out manifests against the schedule they set. A failed inspection can bring a fine or an order to upgrade. We install to pass. That means a sample port where the program wants one and a unit sized to the number their formula gives, not the number the last contractor guessed.

    Zurn Grease Trap Installation in Enid

    Zurn's indoor grease interceptors cover the common sizes from small under-sink units up to 100 GPM, and we set enough of them to know where the gaskets give trouble. After a few years of hot water and scraping, the lid gasket is what usually leaks. We keep spares on the truck. Most Zurn units come with a flow control fitting, and we mount it on the inlet side at the height the instructions call for.
