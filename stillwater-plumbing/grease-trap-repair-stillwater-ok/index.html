    Grease Trap Fixes We Handle in Stillwater, OK

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Stillwater

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. A jetter scrubs the whole pipe wall instead. We run a commercial jetter at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI through the line feeding the trap and the line leaving it, working from the cleanout toward the main. With old cast iron, we turn the pressure down and keep a camera on it, since a thin wall can blow out.

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Stillwater, OK

    Lids crack when someone sets a stockpot on them or a dolly rolls across a floor-mounted unit. A cracked lid lets odor out and lets mop water in. We replace the lid and gasket with the manufacturer part, and on in-floor units we check that the lid sits flush so nobody catches a toe or a cart wheel on it. While the lid is off, we look at the baffles too.

    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Stillwater

    Hardened grease in the inlet tee is the usual culprit when a trap backs up. We pump the trap down, then jet the inlet line with a 4,000 PSI unit until water runs free. Food solids packed against the baffle get scraped out by hand. Then we look for the reason it clogged, such as a dishwasher dumping too much hot water at once or a missing sink strainer upstream. A clog that keeps coming back nearly always has a cause you can see.

    Cost-Effective Grease Trap Solutions in Stillwater, OK

    Pricing depends on what failed, so we quote after the trap is pumped and we can see inside it. Replacing a gasket or a tee is a modest bill. A concrete interceptor with failed walls is a different conversation, and we will lay out repair and replacement side by side with the cost of each and how many years you can expect from them.     Commercial Grease Trap Repair in Stillwater

    Restaurants, school cafeterias, hospital kitchens and bakeries all push more grease than a small trap can hold. We see undersized traps fail in the same few places every time. The inlet tee clogs, the outlet tee gets coated, and grease starts showing up in the sewer manhole down the street, which is usually how the city finds out before the owner does. We fix the failed parts and tell you plainly if the trap is too small for the kitchen.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Stillwater, OK

    Lines downstream of the trap should stay clean if the trap is working. Grease past the outlet is a red flag. We jet the downstream run to the property line or the sewer tap, then camera it to confirm the pipe is clear and to document any cracked or sagging sections for a repair quote. Clearing the pipe without fixing the trap just resets the clock.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Stillwater, OK

    Tees are cheap parts with an outsized job. The inlet tee sends flow down below the grease layer. The outlet tee draws water from low in the tank, well under the grease cap, so if it breaks off or someone removes it during pumping, floating grease walks right out to the sewer. New tees go in as Schedule 40 PVC, cut to the drop length the manufacturer or local code specifies.

    Commercial Grease Interceptor Repair in Stillwater, OK

    In-ground gravity interceptors run from about 500 gallons to several thousand, usually precast concrete with two compartments. Hydrogen sulfide from the waste eats the concrete above the waterline over time. We pump the tank, inspect the walls and the crossover, and repair spalled concrete with a cementitious patch or an epoxy liner rated for that service. Entry follows confined space rules.
