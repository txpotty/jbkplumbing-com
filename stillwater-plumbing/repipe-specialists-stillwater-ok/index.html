    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Stillwater

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Soil movement can separate sewer lines and water lines over time, so foundation shifts and plumbing problems often go together. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Stillwater, OK

    Polybutylene pipes, installed in many homes between the 1970s and 1990s, are known for premature failure due to chemical reactions with disinfectants in the water supply. If your home in Stillwater, OK has polybutylene, a full replacement is essential to prevent catastrophic water damage. JBK Plumbing offers specialized polybutylene plumbing replacement, carefully removing the old, brittle pipes and installing a dependable new system.
