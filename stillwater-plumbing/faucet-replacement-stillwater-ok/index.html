    Utility Sink Faucet Replacement in Stillwater

    Utility faucets in garages and laundry rooms take hard use. People hang buckets on the spout and yank hoses off the threads, which loosens the mounting and cracks the supply connections over time. Brass bodies hold up best here. We mount it tight to the wall or tub deck.

    Bathroom Faucet Replacement in Stillwater, OK

    Old lav faucets rarely come off clean. The mounting nuts sit behind the bowl in a space about the width of a hand, and after twenty years they are usually crusted to the shank. A basin wrench or a cutter gets them off. We replace the pop-up drain at the same time, since the old drain gasket is almost always cracked by then.

    Corroded Faucet Replacement in Stillwater

    Green crust at the base means the faucet body is corroding. A pitted body will not let a new cartridge seal, so the drip comes back a few weeks after each repair. Replacement is the only fix. Even with fused nuts, we get it out and install a faucet with a ceramic disc cartridge.

    Delta Faucet Replacement in Stillwater, OK

    Delta faucets come in a few cartridge families, and the model number tells you which. Older single-handle Delta faucets use a ball valve with seats and springs, while newer ones use a cartridge, and that difference decides whether a repair is worth doing. We tell you which you have. A sound body sometimes only needs parts.

    Commercial Faucet Replacement in Stillwater

    Restaurants lose money every hour a hand sink is down. We replace commercial faucets after close or before open, and we bring the most common pre-rinse and hand sink models so the job finishes in one visit. Lever handles meet ADA rules. Health inspectors check for a working hand sink, so we do those first.

    Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Stillwater

    Kitchen faucets usually fail at the hose first. Pull-down hoses crack at the spot where they flex over the neck, and the leak runs inside the body down into the cabinet where it stays hidden. A hose kit can fix that. If the body is also worn, the whole faucet gets replaced along with the supplies.

    Urgent Faucet Replacement in Stillwater, OK

    Water that will not shut off at the faucet is an urgent call. Turn the shutoff under the sink clockwise, and go to the main if that valve will not budge or starts dripping. We take urgent calls. A plumber stops the flow, removes the broken faucet and installs a new one from the truck.

    Water-Saving Faucet Upgrades in Stillwater

We can swap a bath faucet for a WaterSense model in the same visit.
