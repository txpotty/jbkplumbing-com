    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Stillwater

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in homes built before the 1970s. Over time, it corrodes inside and chokes the pipe. This causes low pressure and rusty water. We replace galvanized lines with modern PEX or copper. The difference in water pressure is often noticeable right away.

    Copper Service Line Installation in Stillwater, OK

    Type K copper is a durable option for underground water lines. It resists punctures and lasts for decades. Copper costs more than PEX but is preferred by some homeowners. We install copper with proper bedding to protect it. We can also install sleeves where it passes through foundations.

    Polybutylene pipe was used in some homes built in the 1980s and 1990s. It can become brittle and fail with no warning. Many insurers flag it as a risk. We replace polybutylene service lines with modern materials. This lowers the chance of sudden leaks.
