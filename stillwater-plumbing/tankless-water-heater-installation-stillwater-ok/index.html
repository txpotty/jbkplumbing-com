    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Stillwater, OK

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Much of Oklahoma has moderately hard to very hard water, especially in western areas, which leaves mineral deposits in water heaters and fixtures. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. Flush it yearly. We install isolation valves for that.
