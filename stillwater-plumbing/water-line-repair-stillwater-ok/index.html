    PEX Water Line Repair in Stillwater, OK

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant option more and more used in homes across Stillwater, OK. While PEX is less prone to leaks than older materials, damage can still occur from improper installation, rodent chewing, or impact. Our team is fully experienced in PEX repair, using specialized crimping tools and fittings to make strong, leak-proof connections. We ensure your PEX system continues to perform well.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Stillwater, OK

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Many rural homes rely on private wells, and some groundwater carries high mineral content that benefits from softening or filtration. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which restricts flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Stillwater, OK

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can work loose those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is responsible for the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve in the same visit.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Stillwater

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a particularly challenging issue. Newer homes around Stillwater typically use PEX supply lines and PVC drains, while older ones may still have galvanized pipe that chokes flow. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to major structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to pinpoint slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak quickly in Stillwater is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Stillwater

    Copper pipes are known for their durability and long life, but they can still develop leaks due to corrosion, excessive water pressure, or physical damage. In Stillwater, we regularly see pinhole leaks in older copper lines. Our experienced plumbers are adept at repairing copper water lines, whether it involves soldering a small patch or replacing a damaged section. We make sure all repairs meet current plumbing standards for a reliable, leak-free connection.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Stillwater

    Whether your home has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our technicians are experienced in repairing both.
