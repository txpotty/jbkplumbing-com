    Tankless vs. Tank Water Heater Conversion in Stillwater, OK

    Thinking about converting from a traditional tank water heater to a tankless system in your Stillwater, OK home? This is a common and worthwhile upgrade, but it requires specialized expertise. We handle the entire conversion, which can involve rerouting gas lines, upgrading electrical service, modifying water lines and installing new venting. Our team makes for a smooth transition, so you can enjoy the long-term benefits of a modern, efficient tankless system.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Stillwater, OK

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can wear out plastic covers and wiring over time. Many homes were not built with deep cold in mind, so pipes in attics, exterior walls and garages are especially vulnerable. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to guard the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the easiest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Stillwater

    Restaurants, salons and small offices often use multiple tankless units linked together on a rack system.     Tank to Tankless Conversions in Stillwater, OK

    Converting from a 40 or 50 gallon tank to tankless frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss in a garage or closet. The catch is that tankless units often need a larger gas line, often three quarter inch or bigger, plus new venting. We check your meter size and pipe run before quoting so there are no surprises mid-job. We also cap or reroute the old flue and seal the roof penetration if it is no longer used. Expect a conversion to take a full day.
