    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Stillwater

    Tankless units are particularly sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures consistent.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Stillwater, OK

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as necessary. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water fast. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Stillwater, OK

    Gas water heater maintenance involves important safety inspections in Stillwater, OK. We check for gas leaks, proper venting of exhaust gases, and the function of the pilot light or igniter. We also clean the burner assembly and inspect the thermocouple. These checks are essential to prevent carbon monoxide exposure and keep your gas-fired appliance running safely.

    Attic and Garage Water Heater Maintenance in Stillwater

    Many homes have water heaters in garages or attics, where heat, cold and tight access add stress to the unit. Attic units need working drain pans and drain lines to prevent ceiling damage if the tank leaks. We check the pan, drain line, venting and access platform. Garage units should be raised and protected where required for gas appliances. We confirm your setup meets local code.
