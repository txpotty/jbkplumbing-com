    Rental and Commercial Property Inspections in Stillwater

    Landlords and property managers use our inspections between tenants or before buying an investment property. Every unit gets its own notes. We note heaters, fixtures and sewers by unit, and commercial jobs can include backflow and grease lines, giving owners a budget for repairs.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Stillwater

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. Warm spots are another clue. Meter tests, pressure loss checks and a look for damp baseboards come first, and slab homes get acoustic and thermal follow-up. Early slab leak finds save floors and foundations.

    Water Heater Inspections in Stillwater

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. The serial number tells us the age, and we check for rust, look at the vent and test the relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. On gas units, we look at the burner and flame, check for a sediment trap and confirm the vent is drafting so combustion gases go outside.

    Pre-Purchase Inspection With Sewer Video in Stillwater

    Plumbing problems are some of the most expensive surprises after closing. Our pre-purchase inspection covers the supply pipe type, the sewer condition on video, the water heater's age and every fixture. Your agent gets the report. Many buyers use our findings to ask for repairs or credits, and the sewer video alone has saved buyers from five-figure surprises.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Stillwater

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. We test the T and P valve, confirm its discharge line runs to a safe place, look for an expansion tank where one is needed and check the drain pan. We check gas units for draft. Backdrafting heaters can push combustion gases indoors, which is why we test the vent and report problems before we leave.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Stillwater, OK

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Stillwater, OK

    Hot summers and sudden temperature swings are the main climate stress in Oklahoma, putting extra strain on pipes, joints and water heaters. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. Outdoor faucets, exposed pipe, irrigation backflow devices and the heater drain and relief valve all get checked. We look at pipe insulation. Fixing a cracked hose bibb or adding insulation is a small job compared to a burst pipe in the wall behind it.
