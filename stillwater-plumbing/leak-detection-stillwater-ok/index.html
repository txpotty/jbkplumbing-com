    Slab Leaks From Ground Movement in Stillwater

    Pipe set in concrete cannot flex when the ground moves, so it cracks at elbows and where it rises through the slab. Soil movement can crack sewer lines and water lines over time, so foundation shifts and plumbing problems often go together. Isolating the line comes first. Then acoustic listening and thermal imaging find the leak so nobody has to break the floor open to search, and you get a marked spot plus repair options before the saw comes out.

    Preventive Leak Inspections in Stillwater, OK

    A yearly leak check catches small problems early. We test static pressure, read the meter with everything off and look at supply lines, water heater fittings and shutoff valves. Older homes with galvanized pipe or original water heater connectors get the most out of this, because those parts often fail together. It is a short visit.

    Water Line Leak Detection in Stillwater, OK

    Supply leaks hide inside walls, above ceilings and under yards. The bill usually tells you first. We read the meter, close fixture stops one at a time and check framing with a moisture meter, then confirm with acoustic gear on pressurized lines, so the repair opening stays small in your Stillwater home.

    Hidden Wall and Ceiling Leaks in Stillwater

    Bubbling paint, soft drywall and a musty smell are the signs we hear about most. All of them mean water. Thermal cameras find the wet area, and a moisture meter confirms it. Acoustic tools help when the leak is on a pressurized line, and once the source is pinned down we cut a single neat access opening instead of chasing the stain across the wall.

    Gas Line Leak Locating in Stillwater

    You cannot locate a gas leak by smell alone. Our plumbers pressure test the system with a gauge, and check every joint with soap and a detector.     Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Stillwater, OK

    The service line from the meter to the house can leak for months without surfacing, especially in old galvanized pipe. A green stripe in a dry yard is a hint. We trace the line with an electronic locator, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak, switching to tracer gas on plastic lines that do not carry sound.

    Pool and Spa Leak Detection in Stillwater

    Heat and wind can pull a lot of water out of a pool, which makes it tricky to separate evaporation from a real leak. A bucket test settles it. We then pressure test the plumbing lines and dye test around lights, skimmers and return fittings, because a running leak can undermine the soil around the pool.
