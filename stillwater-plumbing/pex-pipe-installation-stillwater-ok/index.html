    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Stillwater, OK

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, similar to a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when multiple fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to weigh cost and performance.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Stillwater

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Much of Oklahoma has moderately hard to very hard water, especially in western areas, which leaves scale in water heaters and fixtures. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more often follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and reduces the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.
