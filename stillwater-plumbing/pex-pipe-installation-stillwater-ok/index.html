    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Stillwater

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Either one lasts for decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Stillwater

    Pinhole leaks in copper rarely show up alone.     PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Stillwater, OK

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. All three are code-listed for drinking water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.
