    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Stillwater

    Grit gets in. A small screen at the cold inlet catches sand and scale flakes, especially after a main break. On units with an air intake filter, dust and lint load it up and the burner runs rich or throws a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Recirculation and Combi Unit Service in Stillwater, OK

    Built-in recirculation pumps and buffer tanks get hot water to the tap faster, but they also push more water through the exchanger every day, so those units scale sooner. We flush the recirculation loop along with the exchanger. Then we check whether the system uses a crossover valve or a dedicated return line and set the schedule to match.

    Rinnai Tankless Service in Stillwater

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. A code 11 is a failed ignition, while code 12 is a flame that lit and then dropped out. On a Rinnai visit we clean the air intake filter and check the flame rod for soot or cracks. The LC codes are scale warnings, and they usually clear once the exchanger has been flushed and the unit is reset.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Stillwater, OK

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. Rinnai LC codes point to scale, Navien E003 is a failed ignition and Noritz 90 points at combustion air or exhaust trouble. We pull the code history before touching anything. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Tankless Banks for Restaurants and Apartments in Stillwater

    Cascade systems link several tankless units to one controller that fires them in turn. They see heavy use, and scale builds faster than at home. We descale each heater in the bank, check the cascade controller and confirm the load is shared evenly across every unit. Quarterly service is common.

    Commercial Tankless Maintenance in Stillwater, OK

    Commercial kitchens and salons burn through hot water all day, so their tankless units scale up much faster than a house unit. Gas pressure gets checked with every burner lit, not at idle. Inlet screens and air filters get pulled and cleaned. Downtime is the real cost. So we schedule the work before opening or after close.

    Same-Day Tankless Help in Stillwater, OK

    A blinking error code and a cold shower usually mean the heater protected itself from something. Usually it is a blocked exhaust or heavy scale. We bring common parts and a descaling kit on emergency calls, so most heaters are firing again before we pack up the truck.

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Stillwater, OK
