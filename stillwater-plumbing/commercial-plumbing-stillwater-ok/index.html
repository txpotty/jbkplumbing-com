    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Stillwater

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. Our jetters push water at 3,000 to 4,000 psi through a spinning nozzle that scours the full pipe wall, which clears 4-inch and 6-inch lines back to bare pipe. A jetted line stays open far longer than a cabled one. Restaurants, schools and apartment stacks are where we see the biggest difference.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Stillwater

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. Our certified testers check reduced pressure zone assemblies, double check valves and pressure vacuum breakers with calibrated differential gauges, then file the report with your water provider. Failed devices usually need a rebuild kit, not a new valve. We carry Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits for that reason.

    After-Hours Commercial Plumbing in Stillwater

    Emergencies at a business cost more the longer they last, so speed matters more than the hourly rate. Water on the floor comes first.     Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in Stillwater, OK

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while. Once leaks start showing up in different spots every few months, a sectional repipe in Type L copper or commercial-grade PEX usually costs less than the next three emergency calls. The first step is mapping the risers and branches. Then we phase the work by floor or by tenant so the building stays open.

    Commercial Sewer Line Repair in Stillwater, OK

    The building sewer on a commercial property carries everything from every tenant, so a root intrusion or a belly in the line affects the whole property. First we run a camera and locate the damage depth, then we compare a spot dig, a cured-in-place liner and pipe bursting. Trenchless saves parking lots. Many 6-inch laterals can be lined in a single day without cutting the parking lot or closing a door.

    Commercial Leak Detection in Stillwater, OK

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. Zone-by-zone shutoffs and meter readings narrow it down, then acoustic sensors, thermal imaging and tracer gas pinpoint the spot. Sometimes the culprit is just a running toilet. A single stuck flush valve can burn through thousands of gallons in a week.

    Food service sites need a grease trap or interceptor to keep fats, oils and grease out of the public sewer, and pretreatment inspectors check them. Odor is the first warning. We repair broken baffles, replace cracked lids and outlet tees, jet the inlet lines and confirm the unit is sized for your fixture count and flow rate. Slow sinks and overflow usually point to a full or damaged trap. Keeping the pumping log current helps at inspection time.
