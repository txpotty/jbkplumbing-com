    Push-Button Drain Installation in Stillwater, OK

    Push-button drains get rid of the lift rod. They open and close with a press on the cap, and we check that the sink has an overflow before choosing a slotted body, since a slotted drain in a bowl without an overflow leaks out the slots. Cleaning is simpler with no linkage. Hair still collects, so pull the cap now and then.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Stillwater

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. The putty under a 3-1/2 inch strainer dries out, the locknut rusts, and water seeps down the outside of the tailpiece where it looks like the trap is leaking even when it is not. Strainer and trap get swapped as a pair. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Leaking P-Trap Replacement in Stillwater

    Traps leak at the joints first. The beveled washers inside the slip nuts flatten and harden over the years, and a trap that has been taken apart several times to clear clogs often has stripped plastic threads or a cracked nut. A new trap kit costs little. Labor is most of the bill.

    Hard-Water Drain Damage Repair in Stillwater, OK

    Homeowners around Stillwater with hard water often add a softener to protect water heaters and appliances. Scale grows inside pop-up bodies and on stopper rods until the linkage seizes.
