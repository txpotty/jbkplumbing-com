    Trenchless Sewer Replacement in Stillwater, OK

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Stillwater

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. The crew trenches the full length of the lateral, removes the failed pipe and sets new PVC on compacted bedding with steady fall.     Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Stillwater, OK

    Concrete is expensive to replace. Bursting lets us run new HDPE under a driveway, patio or mature tree by pulling it through the path of the old pipe from a launch pit to a receiving pit. The HDPE is butt-fused on site into one long piece. A typical burst takes one working day. You end up patching two small pits rather than a whole slab.

    Emergency Sewer Replacement in Stillwater, OK

    Orangeburg is tar-soaked wood fiber. Many mid-century houses got it, and by now most of it has blistered and flattened enough to snag waste. Clay is tougher but leaks roots. Several bad spots on one run usually make full replacement the better buy.
