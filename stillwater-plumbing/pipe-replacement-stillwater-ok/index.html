    Copper Pipe Replacement in Stillwater, OK

    Copper piping is a long-standing, proven choice known for its durability and resistance to corrosion. While it's a premium material, its longevity often makes it a cost-effective solution over time. JBK Plumbing offers expert copper pipe replacement for sections or entire homes in Stillwater, OK. Our plumbers are skilled in the careful soldering and installation techniques required for copper, ensuring a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that holds up year after year.

    PEX Repiping in Stillwater

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for better flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with far fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most requested repipe material.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair and Replacement in Stillwater

    Slab leaks, where pipes under your concrete foundation leak, are a serious concern for homeowners in Stillwater. These leaks can cause foundation damage, mold growth, and wasted water. JBK Plumbing uses modern leak detection technology to pinpoint the exact location of the leak. Depending on the severity and accessibility, we offer targeted pipe repair or rerouting and replacement solutions to fully resolve the issue, protecting your home's structural integrity.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Stillwater, OK

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep coming back. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you compare the cost of both paths.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Stillwater, OK

    Many older homes in Stillwater, OK still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to internal corrosion over time.     Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Stillwater

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack without warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when listing the home.
