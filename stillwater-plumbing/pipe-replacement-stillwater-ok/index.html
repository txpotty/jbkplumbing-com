    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Stillwater

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. Chlorine breaks it down over time. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, which can cause insurance headaches. Replace it.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Stillwater

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. Before a cold front, homeowners around Stillwater should disconnect hoses, cover outdoor spigots and let faucets drip overnight. It often survives freezing where copper would crack. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, keeping flow strong at each joint. Faster install.

    Full Repipes for Older Homes in Stillwater

    Homes from the 1950s and 1960s often still have original galvanized pipe. That pipe is worn out. A full repipe brings new PEX or copper to every fixture, and fixes pressure and water color at once. Worth it.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Stillwater, OK

    Drain lines fail too. Old cast iron rusts through. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, and making sure vents and fall are correct. Camera first.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair and Replacement in Stillwater

    Water under a slab shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a sound of running water, or a meter that spins with everything off. We locate the leak first.     Whole-Home Re-Piping in Stillwater
