    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Stillwater

    A high water alarm should be on any ejector basin, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. We install audible alarms with their own power source, or Wi-Fi alarms that text your phone, so you hear about a stuck float while the basin is still filling and not after waste has reached the basement floor. Storm-related power outages can stop sump pumps and well pumps, so a standby power plan is worth having. Backup power can help.
