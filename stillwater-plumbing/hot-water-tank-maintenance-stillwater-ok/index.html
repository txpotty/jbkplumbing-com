    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Stillwater, OK

    The anode rod is a sacrificial metal rod that corrodes so the steel tank does not.     Relief Valve Testing in Stillwater

    Temperature and pressure relief valves on residential tanks are typically rated to open at 150 psi or 210 degrees, and they can stick shut after years of mineral buildup. Our tech lifts the lever, lets water flow, then watches it close. If it keeps dripping, we replace it.     Anode Upgrades for Odor in Stillwater, OK

    A rotten egg smell from the hot side only is almost always sulfate-reducing bacteria reacting with a magnesium anode. Swapping the rod usually fixes it. We put in an aluminum-zinc or powered anode and sanitize the tank. Powered anodes never wear out and draw very little electricity.

    Sediment Flushing in Stillwater, OK

    Sediment is mostly calcium carbonate and sand, and it settles in a layer that can reach several inches on a neglected 50-gallon tank. That layer insulates water from the heat.
