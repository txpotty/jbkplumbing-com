    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Stillwater

    Leaks at the base, rusty water, rumbling noises, inconsistent hot water and an age over 10 years are typical signs. A leaking tank should be replaced right away.

    New water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and parts, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. JBK Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, giving you added peace of mind. Specific warranty details vary by brand and model.

    Flushing a traditional tank water heater once a year is recommended in most homes. Homeowners around Stillwater with hard water often add a softener to extend the life of water heaters and appliances.     Choosing the right size depends on the number of people in your household, their hot water usage habits and peak demand times.
