    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Stillwater

    Disc Faucet Repair in Stillwater

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. Inlet seals cost very little. A scratched disc means a new cartridge, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Stillwater, OK

    A new cartridge usually costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life, as long as the faucet body is sound and parts are still made. Cracked or discontinued faucets are different. We give you both prices so the choice is yours.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Stillwater

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. Flushing usually clears it. Pull-down faucets also have check valves in the hose that can stick, and we clean or replace those.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Stillwater, OK

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. A diverter costs very little. Touchless faucets add a solenoid and a battery pack to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Stillwater
