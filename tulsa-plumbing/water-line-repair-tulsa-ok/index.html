    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Tulsa, OK

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Two or more leaks on galvanized is a replacement.     Trenchless Water Line Repair in Tulsa

    Trenchless work makes sense when the line runs under a driveway, patio or a row of mature shrubs. We dig a pit at the meter and another at the house. A pneumatic boring tool or a cable puller drags HDPE or PEX through the soil, or through the old pipe if it is a straight run, usually in one working day from start to backfill.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Tulsa

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. Straight runs are ideal. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, while new HDPE comes through right behind the head. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.
