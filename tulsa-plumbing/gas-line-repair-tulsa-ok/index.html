    Propane Gas Line Service in Tulsa, OK

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too.     Gas Leak Detection in Tulsa

    A rotten egg smell, hissing near a line, dead grass in a stripe across the yard or a gas meter that moves with everything off are the common leak signs. Trust your nose. After we locate the leak, we price the repair in writing.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Tulsa
