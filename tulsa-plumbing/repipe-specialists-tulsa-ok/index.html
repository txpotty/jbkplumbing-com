    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Tulsa

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Expansive red clay is common in Oklahoma, swelling when wet and shrinking in drought, which shifts foundations and buried pipe. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.
