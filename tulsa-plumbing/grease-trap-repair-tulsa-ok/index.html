    Damaged Baffle Repair in Tulsa

    The baffles inside a grease trap are critical for separating FOGs from wastewater. If these baffles are corroded, broken, or dislodged, the trap's efficiency plummets, allowing grease into the sewer system. Our service includes inspecting the condition of all baffles and repairing or replacing them as needed, often using durable, corrosion-resistant materials. Proper baffle function is key to compliance with wastewater regulations in Tulsa.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Tulsa

    When traditional snaking isn't enough, hydro-jetting is our go-to method for severe grease trap blockages. It's a very effective, non-invasive method that not only clears clogs but also cleans the lines, significantly reducing the likelihood of future blockages in your Tulsa business.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Tulsa, OK

    Tees direct flow into and out of the trap at the correct depth. Missing or broken tees allow floating grease to escape into the sewer line. We replace damaged tees with durable PVC or other approved materials. We also check that they are set at the right depth for your unit. This is one of the most common and important grease trap repairs.

    Flow Control Device Repair in Tulsa

    Hydromechanical traps rely on a flow control fitting to keep water moving at the rated speed. If this fitting is missing or clogged, the trap can be overwhelmed and grease escapes. We inspect, clean or replace flow control devices during repair visits. We also check the air intake that allows the device to work properly. Correct flow control is essential for a trap to pass inspection.
