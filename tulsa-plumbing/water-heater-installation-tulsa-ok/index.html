    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Tulsa

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. We install a new flex connector with a sediment trap, check vent pitch and draft and test every joint with a gas leak detector. Venting is what keeps CO out. All work follows local code.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Tulsa

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Oklahoma enforces a statewide plumbing code based on a national model code, and plumbers must be licensed by the state. A small expansion tank on the cold line absorbs that extra volume, which keeps the relief valve from weeping and saves wear on faucets and fittings.     Gas Water Heater Installation in Tulsa, OK

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour.     Tankless Water Heater Installation in Tulsa, OK
