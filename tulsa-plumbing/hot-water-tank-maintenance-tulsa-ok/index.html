    Standard Hot Water Tank Tune-Up in Tulsa

    JBK Plumbing offers a complete tune-up for conventional hot water tanks, focusing on critical components. This includes draining the tank to remove sediment buildup, which is essential wherever water carries minerals, and inspecting the anode rod for signs of corrosion. We also check heating elements, thermostat settings and all pipe connections for leaks. Our goal is to improve your tank's performance and prevent future issues.

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Tulsa

    A properly functioning temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to ensure it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will suggest immediate replacement to keep your Tulsa home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Tulsa, OK

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is vital for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it changes the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's badly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, saving you from a full tank replacement.

    Electric Hot Water Tank Element Check in Tulsa

    Electric hot water tanks rely on heating elements to warm the water. During maintenance, our plumbers inspect these elements for signs of wear, corrosion or burnout. We also check the thermostat settings and wiring connections to confirm they are functioning correctly and safely. This preventative check can identify failing elements before they stop heating altogether, avoiding unexpected cold showers for your family in Tulsa.

    Emergency Hot Water Tank Inspections in Tulsa

    If you notice sudden changes in your hot water tank's performance, like strange noises, discolored water or a sudden drop in hot water supply, JBK Plumbing offers emergency inspections in Tulsa. While not a full maintenance, we can quickly diagnose the issue and advise on immediate steps. That often includes a focused maintenance procedure to restore function and prevent further damage.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Tulsa, OK

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can clog the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a steady blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Electric Element and Thermostat Service in Tulsa

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. We test each element with a meter, check the thermostats and look for scorched wires at the terminal block. If an element has failed we replace it with the matching wattage and voltage. Plenty of families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.

    Standard Hot Water Tank Flushing in Tulsa

    Sediment buildup is a common issue in hot water tanks, especially where water hardness runs high. Our standard flushing service removes these mineral deposits, which can reduce efficiency and lead to premature tank failure. We drain the tank completely, flush out all sediment and refill it, giving you clear water flow and improved heating performance. This routine service is key for extending the life of your unit.
