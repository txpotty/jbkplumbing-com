    Slab Leak Detection in Tulsa

    Slab leaks are a common concern for homes with concrete foundations, especially where soils shift. Our technicians use specialized acoustic equipment and thermal cameras to pinpoint leaks under your home's foundation without destructive digging. Early detection of a slab leak prevents serious structural damage and mold issues, saving you significant repair costs down the line. We can locate leaks in both hot and cold water lines under the slab.

    Irrigation and Yard Leak Detection in Tulsa, OK

    Drip and sprinkler systems are a common source of hidden water loss in most yards. A cracked lateral or a stuck valve can waste thousands of gallons before anyone notices. We test the irrigation system separately from the house and trace leaks along buried lines. Many water providers recommend checking irrigation first when bills spike in summer.

    Pool and Spa Leak Detection in Tulsa

    Summer heat and wind can evaporate a lot of pool water, so it can be hard to tell normal loss from a leak. A bucket test compares evaporation to actual water loss. We then pressure test plumbing lines and use dye testing around lights, skimmers and fittings. Finding the leak protects the pool shell and the soil around it.
