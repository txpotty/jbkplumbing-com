    Sewer Camera Inspection in Tulsa

    Every sewer repair starts with a look inside the pipe. We run a self-leveling camera from your cleanout or a pulled toilet and record the full length of the line. A sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact location and depth of every problem on the surface. You get a copy of the video and a plain-English summary of what we found.     Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Tulsa

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can usually use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Tulsa, OK

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive solution. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from major disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an efficient and popular choice for preserving property appearance in Tulsa.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Tulsa, OK

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is well suited for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Tulsa.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Tulsa

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to recurring clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it holds.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Tulsa, OK

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Expansive red clay is common in Oklahoma, swelling when wet and shrinking in drought, which shifts foundations and buried pipe. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is usually a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Tulsa, OK

    A single spot repair is the most affordable fix when the rest of the line is sound. Pipe lining costs more per foot but avoids excavation and works well for long runs under driveways or landscaping. Full replacement is the right call when the pipe is Orangeburg, badly bellied or broken in several places. We lay out all three options with real numbers so you can weigh cost against how long the repair will last.
