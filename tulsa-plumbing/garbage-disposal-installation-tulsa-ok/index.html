    A 1 HP disposal earns its keep in a busy kitchen, like an InSinkErator Evolution or a Waste King Legend model.     Same-Day Disposal Installation in Tulsa, OK

    If your old disposal quits with a sink full of water, we can usually install a new one the same day. We carry 1/2 and 3/4 HP units, mounts and cord kits on the truck, so there is no supply house run before your sink drains. Figure on roughly an hour.

    Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Tulsa

    Every new disposal has a plug sealing the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp. A plug left in backs up the dishwasher. An air gap or high loop goes in too, which keeps sink water from running back into the dishwasher.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Installation in Tulsa, OK

    Batch-feed units only run with the lid in place, and the lid holds a magnet that closes a switch inside the unit. No wall switch is needed, which makes them handy in kitchens where running a new switch would mean opening finished walls. You grind one load at a time. Parents of young kids often think that is worth it.
