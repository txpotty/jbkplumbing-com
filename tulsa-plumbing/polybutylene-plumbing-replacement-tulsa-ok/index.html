    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in Tulsa

    Polybutylene's built-in weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Much of Oklahoma has moderately hard to very hard water, especially in western areas, which leaves scale in water heaters and fixtures. Hard water can also add stress to pipe that is already weakened. Understanding this failure mechanism is important for diagnosing issues and recommending the most effective and permanent replacement strategy for your home.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Tulsa

    When a home with polybutylene pipes sits on a slab foundation, repiping becomes a more complex process. Our team specializes in full home repiping using PEX (cross-linked polyethylene), a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant material. PEX is an excellent choice for Oklahoma homes, as it resists freezing better than rigid pipes and expands to accommodate temperature changes. We carefully plan the repipe to limit disruption, often running new lines through attics or existing wall cavities, ensuring a complete and long-lasting upgrade for your entire plumbing system.
